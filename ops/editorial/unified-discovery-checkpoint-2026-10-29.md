## User-approved mission
One TexasDefined original authority identity per permanent attraction, annual
event, dated occurrence and recurring experience. Use Viator, official sources,
Ticketmaster and existing event feeds for discovery and booking; integrate with
Explore, statewide and seasonal calendars, county/city/event pages. Preserve
original-source authority, SEO, affiliate tracking and crosslinks. Future
Group Events & Experiences marketplace is a separate consumer of the data
model; do not alter its deferred account/venue portal scope.

## Current implementation on branch
- `src/data/discovery/entity-registry.ts`: canonical source/evidence,
  occurrence, booking relationships, official dates and indexing safeguards.
- `src/data/discovery/attraction-triage.ts`: names/IDs are candidates; matches
  to existing Top-25 guides require review, never auto-publish.
- `src/data/discovery/explore-attraction-guides.ts`: existing original
  attractions featured per reviewed Explore market.
- `src/data/discovery/market-event-cities.ts`: exact city filters into
  verified statewide calendar without inventing ambiguous regional matches.
- `src/lib/viator-attraction-discovery.ts` and `src/server.ts`: one-week
  cached, bounded, robots-blocked Viator attraction research endpoint for
  approved Texas markets.
- `src/lib/viator-seasonal-discovery.ts` and `src/server.ts`: bounded
  Christmas `search/freetext` query returning separate supplier product
  previews, not organizer-verified dated events.
- `src/components/events/CalendarBookingDiscovery.tsx`: opt-in holiday and
  general experiences on statewide and evergreen event calendar pages.
- Explore market cards link original attraction guides + upcoming verified
  city events + existing, sponsored Viator market/products.
- `src/data/discovery/canonical-links.ts`: event and county relationships;
  statewide/event collection results now link applicable counties.
- `public/robots.txt`: explicit disallow `/api/viator/` in all crawler
  groups to protect supplier-only data.
- Weekly `.github/workflows/weekly-viator-attraction-discovery.yml`:
  bounded 14-market candidate sample to internal short-lived Actions artifact.
  Neither descriptions nor new authority pages are autogenerated.
- Tests and `scripts/data/validate-unified-discovery-governance.mjs` wired
  into PR and production predeploy gates.
