# 10 Texas Zoos and Aquariums Worth Planning a Family Trip Around

Texas has enough major zoological institutions that families do not need a fake statewide “best” ranking. A better guide asks what kind of experience you want: a huge urban zoo, a drive-through conservation center, a Gulf Coast aquarium, a South Texas collection, or a smaller regional zoo that can anchor a weekend.

For this draft, the strongest initial filter is current accreditation by the Association of Zoos and Aquariums (AZA). Accreditation can change, so verify every institution again immediately before publication.

## 1. Fort Worth Zoo — Fort Worth

The Fort Worth Zoo is one of the state's major destination zoos and is currently listed by AZA as accredited through March 2029.

Why plan around it: a large collection, major exhibits and a location that can be combined with Fort Worth's museums, Stockyards and family attractions.

## 2. Houston Zoo — Houston

The Houston Zoo is currently AZA-accredited through March 2029 and sits inside Hermann Park near several major museums.

Why plan around it: the zoo can be paired with the Museum District, making it practical for a multi-day family trip without spending every day in the car.

## 3. Dallas Zoo — Dallas

Dallas Zoo is currently AZA-accredited through March 2028.

Why plan around it: a major urban zoo with easy connections to a broader Dallas itinerary and public-transit access that some Texas attractions lack.

## 4. San Antonio Zoo — San Antonio

The San Antonio Zoological Society is currently AZA-accredited through March 2030.

Why plan around it: the zoo sits within the broader Brackenridge Park area, allowing families to combine animals with other central San Antonio attractions.

## 5. Gladys Porter Zoo — Brownsville

Gladys Porter Zoo is currently AZA-accredited through September 2027.

Why plan around it: the Brownsville location creates a South Texas/Lower Rio Grande Valley trip that can also include birding, wildlife refuges, Gulf Coast destinations and borderland culture.

## 6. Cameron Park Zoo — Waco

Cameron Park Zoo is currently AZA-accredited through March 2027.

Why plan around it: a manageable regional zoo that pairs naturally with Waco's other family attractions and the Brazos River/Cameron Park area.

## 7. Caldwell Zoo — Tyler

Caldwell Zoo is currently AZA-accredited through March 2031.

Why plan around it: it gives East Texas families a serious zoological destination without requiring a Dallas or Houston trip and can anchor a Piney Woods weekend.

## 8. Fossil Rim Wildlife Center — Glen Rose

Fossil Rim Wildlife Center is currently AZA-accredited through March 2030.

Why plan around it: the experience is different from a traditional walk-through city zoo. Its drive-through wildlife format can be paired with Dinosaur Valley State Park for a particularly strong family weekend in Somervell County.

## 9. Moody Gardens Rainforest and Aquarium — Galveston

Moody Gardens Rainforest and Aquarium is currently AZA-accredited through March 2029.

Why plan around it: the aquarium/rainforest experience fits naturally into a Galveston trip and gives families a weather-resistant alternative when beach conditions are poor.

## 10. Dallas World Aquarium — Dallas

Dallas World Aquarium is currently AZA-accredited through March 2028.

Why plan around it: it offers a different, more indoor and immersive experience than Dallas Zoo and can fit into a downtown Dallas itinerary.

## Other currently accredited Texas institutions worth checking

AZA's current Texas list also includes Abilene Zoological Gardens, Baylor University Bear Habitat, Landry's Houston Aquarium, Longneck Manor in Fredericksburg, SEA LIFE Grapevine Aquarium and SeaWorld San Antonio, among others.

Those may fit a particular trip better than one of the ten above. The list should expand into regional pages rather than pretending ten institutions are the only worthwhile choices.

## What AZA accreditation does—and does not—tell you

AZA accreditation evaluates institutions against professional standards involving animal care, welfare, veterinary programs, conservation, education, safety and operations. It is a useful quality-control signal.

It does not tell a family whether a facility is affordable, uncrowded, stroller-friendly or the right size for a three-year-old. Those are separate trip-planning questions.

## Family trip checklist

Before leaving home, check:

- current operating days/hours;
- timed-entry or reservation requirements;
- parking cost and distance;
- extreme-heat plan and indoor spaces;
- stroller/wagon rules;
- outside-food policies;
- animal/exhibit closures;
- special-event surcharges;
- accessibility information;
- whether the institution's current accreditation/status has changed.

Texas summers make shade, hydration and indoor breaks part of the itinerary rather than an afterthought.

## Build regional combinations

- **DFW:** Fort Worth Zoo + Dallas Zoo/Dallas World Aquarium + museums.
- **Houston/Galveston:** Houston Zoo + Moody Gardens + Gulf Coast attractions.
- **Waco/Glen Rose:** Cameron Park Zoo + Fossil Rim + Dinosaur Valley.
- **East Texas:** Caldwell Zoo + Tyler/Piney Woods destinations.
- **San Antonio:** San Antonio Zoo + historic/cultural attractions.
- **Lower Rio Grande Valley:** Gladys Porter Zoo + birding/refuges + Gulf Coast destinations.
