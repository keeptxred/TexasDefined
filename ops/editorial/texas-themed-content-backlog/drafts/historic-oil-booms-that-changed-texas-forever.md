# Historic Oil Booms That Changed Texas Forever

Texas oil history is not one Spindletop story. Repeated discoveries changed towns, transportation, banking, universities, state revenue and the physical landscape.

## Boom eras to explain

### Corsicana
Late-19th-century discoveries helped establish an early Texas petroleum industry before Spindletop.

### Spindletop and Beaumont
The 1901 Lucas gusher accelerated industrial-scale oil development and helped transform the Gulf Coast economy.

### North Texas fields
Electra, Burkburnett and Wichita Falls became associated with early-20th-century boomtown growth and speculation.

### Ranger and North Central Texas
World War I-era demand and new discoveries reshaped towns and infrastructure.

### East Texas Oil Field
The enormous 1930 discovery brought production, price crashes, proration battles and stronger state regulation.

### Permian Basin
West Texas oil development unfolded across multiple waves and technologies rather than one single boom.

### Shale-era expansion
Horizontal drilling and hydraulic fracturing created another major cycle in the Permian and Eagle Ford, but this section requires current data if promoted.
