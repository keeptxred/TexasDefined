# Major Texas Shopping Districts and Malls to Know

Texas retail ranges from enclosed destination malls to walkable historic districts, luxury corridors, outlet centers and mixed-use neighborhoods. A useful guide should compare **type of experience**, not pretend one statewide ranking fits every shopper.

## 1. NorthPark Center — Dallas

A major Dallas shopping destination known for luxury retail, art and large-scale enclosed-mall design. Store mix and hours require current verification.

## 2. Galleria Dallas — Dallas

Another large North Texas destination combining shopping, dining and entertainment. Avoid hard-coded tenant lists.

## 3. Highland Park Village — Dallas

An open-air luxury retail district with historic architecture and a different experience from conventional malls.

## 4. Fort Worth Stockyards retail district

Western wear, boots, hats, gifts and entertainment make the Stockyards a highly Texas-specific shopping environment, though it is also a major tourist district.

## 5. The Domain — Austin

The Domain represents Austin’s mixed-use, outdoor retail model with restaurants, hotels, offices and shopping integrated into one district.

## 6. South Congress — Austin

South Congress is more street-oriented and mixes boutiques, food, music and tourism. The tenant mix changes frequently.

## 7. The Galleria — Houston

Houston’s Galleria remains one of the state’s best-known large shopping complexes, but store counts and tenant claims should be refreshed annually.

## 8. Houston Heights / 19th Street

Historic storefronts, independent shops and walkability create a smaller-scale alternative to regional malls.

## 9. River Oaks District — Houston

Luxury retail and dining in an outdoor district format. Current brands/tenants require direct verification.

## 10. The Woodlands Town Center

The Woodlands combines mall, outdoor retail and mixed-use zones north of Houston.

## 11. The Shops at La Cantera — San Antonio

A major open-air shopping destination in Northwest San Antonio.

## 12. Historic Market Square — San Antonio

Market Square offers a cultural/market experience very different from a luxury center and ties naturally to downtown San Antonio travel content.

## 13. San Marcos Premium Outlets / Tanger area

San Marcos is a major outlet-shopping destination on the Austin–San Antonio corridor. Store lists and center branding require current verification.

## 14. Grapevine Mills — Grapevine

A large North Texas shopping/entertainment complex that can fit family and airport-area itineraries.

## 15. Smaller downtown shopping districts

Fredericksburg, Wimberley, Round Top, Granbury and other small towns can offer antique, boutique or maker-focused shopping. These should be framed as different experiences, not miniature versions of metro malls.

## How to choose

Choose by intent:

- luxury brands;
- outlet shopping;
- independent boutiques;
- western wear;
- antiques;
- family entertainment;
- walkable downtown experience;
- proximity to a larger travel itinerary.
