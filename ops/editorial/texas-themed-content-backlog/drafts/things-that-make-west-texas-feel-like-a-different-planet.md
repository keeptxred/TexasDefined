# Things That Make West Texas Feel Like a Different Planet

“Different planet” is playful travel language. The article should connect the feeling to real geography instead of pretending West Texas is empty wasteland.

## What creates the effect

- Chihuahuan Desert basins and mountain ranges
- Long sightlines with very little tree cover in some areas
- Volcanic and igneous landforms around parts of the Trans-Pecos
- Dark night skies far from major cities
- Sudden elevation changes in the Davis and Guadalupe mountains
- Dry washes that can become dangerous during flash floods
- Ranch roads and highways with long gaps between services
- Salt flats and pale desert surfaces
- Ocotillo, yucca, agave and other distinctive vegetation
- Extreme temperature swings between sun, shade, day and night
- Borderland culture that is inseparable from the region’s history
- Tiny towns separated by enormous landscapes
- Art and architecture that deliberately use desert scale
- Geological exposures with little vegetation hiding them
- A feeling of distance that changes how travelers plan fuel, water and time
