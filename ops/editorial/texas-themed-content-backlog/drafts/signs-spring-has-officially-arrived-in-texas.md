# Signs Spring Has Arrived in Texas

Texas spring does not arrive on one schedule. South Texas, the Gulf Coast, Central Texas, the Panhandle and mountain country can be weeks apart, so this should be a regional seasonal guide rather than a fixed calendar.

## Signs to watch for

1. Bluebonnets and other early wildflowers begin appearing where rainfall and temperature cooperated.
2. Redbuds, Mexican plums and other spring-blooming trees start changing streets and trails.
3. Migratory birds move through Gulf Coast and inland flyways.
4. Oak pollen and other allergens become impossible to ignore in many regions.
5. Thunderstorm and hail season becomes more active across parts of the state.
6. State-park weekends and popular wildflower routes begin filling earlier.
7. Farmers markets shift toward spring produce.
8. Baseball, festivals and outdoor events crowd local calendars.
9. Warm afternoons can coexist with sharp cold fronts.
10. Gardeners begin watching soil temperature and local frost risk rather than relying on a statewide planting date.
