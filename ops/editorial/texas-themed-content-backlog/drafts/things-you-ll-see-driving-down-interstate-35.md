# Things You’ll See Driving Down Interstate 35 in Texas

Interstate 35 is a moving cross-section of Central and North Texas. Construction and development change constantly, so this should emphasize durable corridor patterns rather than a fixed exit-by-exit guide.

## Corridor scenes

- Laredo freight and border-commerce traffic
- San Antonio’s expanding metro landscape
- New Braunfels and the I-35 growth corridor
- Austin skyline and major interchange construction
- Round Rock/Georgetown suburban expansion
- Temple and Belton corridor growth
- Waco landmarks and Brazos crossings
- Waxahachie and the transition into DFW
- Dallas/Fort Worth split decisions at I-35E and I-35W
- Large distribution centers and logistics facilities
- Buc-ee’s/major travel-stop culture near the corridor
- Billboard-heavy commercial strips
- Construction zones that migrate year to year
- Wildflower views in season where safe/legal to observe
- A very visible demonstration of Texas population growth

## Driving rule

No sightseeing detail is worth distracted driving. Passengers can spot landmarks; drivers should follow signs and current TxDOT traffic guidance.
