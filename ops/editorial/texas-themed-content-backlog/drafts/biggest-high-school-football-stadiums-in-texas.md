# Big Texas High School Football Stadiums Worth Knowing

Texas is famous for large high-school football stadiums, but capacity numbers are easy to repeat incorrectly because districts renovate, add seating, share venues and publish different figures.

The promoted version should use **current district or venue sources for every capacity** and avoid treating stadium size as a ranking of students or programs.

## Stadiums to verify for the final list

### Memorial Stadium — Mesquite
A long-standing large multi-school district stadium in North Texas.

### Eagle Stadium — Allen
One of the best-known modern single-district high-school stadiums. Current capacity and renovation status require Allen ISD verification.

### McKinney ISD Stadium — McKinney
A major modern district venue serving McKinney-area schools.

### Legacy Stadium — Katy
Katy ISD’s stadium complex is a useful example of football scale in a rapidly growing suburban district.

### Cy-Fair FCU Stadium / Berry Center complex — Cypress
Cy-Fair ISD’s large stadium/complex reflects Houston suburban growth and multi-school use.

### Children's Health Stadium / Prosper ISD facilities
Prosper-area growth has produced major new athletics facilities; current names, sponsorships and capacities require district verification.

### Ratliff Stadium — Odessa
West Texas football history makes Ratliff culturally important beyond raw capacity.

### Shotwell Stadium — Abilene
A major West Texas school/community venue with long football history.

### San Angelo Stadium — San Angelo
Another regional stadium where high-school football intersects with broader community events.

### Buccaneer Stadium — Corpus Christi
A South Texas example of a large district stadium used by multiple schools.

## Why Texas districts build shared stadiums

Large districts may use one stadium for several high schools, spreading capital and operating costs across multiple programs. Capacity therefore does not mean one school routinely fills every seat.

## Stadium size is only one story

Texas football culture also includes:

- six-man fields in small communities;
- historic WPA-era stadiums;
- multi-school suburban complexes;
- college venues used for playoffs;
- marching-band and drill-team facilities;
- neutral-site postseason games.

## Current championship context

UIL currently schedules the 2026 football state championships at AT&T Stadium in Arlington. That is a postseason venue, not a high-school-owned stadium, so it should not be mixed into a “biggest high school stadium” ranking.
