# Fashion Staples You May See at a Texas Country Wedding

Country weddings in Texas range from working ranches to polished venues and city events with western details. This list should describe common style choices without implying a dress code for all Texas weddings.

## Common elements

- Cowboy boots paired with dresses or suits
- Felt or straw hats depending on season and formality
- Tooled leather belts or subtle western buckles
- Pearl-snap or western-cut shirts at casual events
- Lace, denim or suede accents used selectively
- Turquoise or silver jewelry
- Floral choices tied to native or Hill Country palettes
- Practical footwear for grass, gravel or ranch terrain
- Lightweight fabrics for warm-weather ceremonies
- Jackets, wraps or coats for winter and high-plains weddings
- Vintage family western pieces
- Groomsmen accessories that nod to ranch or rodeo culture

## Etiquette matters more than theme

Guests should follow the invitation’s dress code. A black-tie Texas wedding is still black tie; “Texas” does not automatically mean jeans and boots.
