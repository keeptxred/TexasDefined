# Oversized Texas Landmarks and “World’s Largest” Attractions

Texas roadside culture loves giant objects, but “world’s largest” claims change and are often promotional. This list should separate measurable records from playful local slogans.

## Types of oversized landmarks to research

- Giant cowboy boots and western-wear sculptures
- Oversized chairs and rocking chairs
- Giant pecans and food-themed monuments
- Large roadside animals
- Huge crosses, stars and civic symbols
- Oversized sports or music objects
- Giant retail/store mascots
- Record-attempt sculptures with documented dimensions

## Verification rule

For every “largest” claim, record dimensions, date measured, comparison category and source. If no recognized record authority exists, phrase it as “billed as,” “locally promoted as” or simply describe the object without a superlative.

## Road-trip safety

Use legal parking and public viewing areas. Do not encourage shoulder stops or entry onto private commercial property outside posted hours.
