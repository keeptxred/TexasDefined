# Facts About the Texas State Guard and Its Military History

The Texas State Guard is easy to confuse with the Texas Army National Guard or Texas Air National Guard, but it is a distinct state defense force within the Texas Military Department.

This article should keep command structure, current mission and historical lineage separate rather than blending every Texas military organization into one story.

## 1. The Texas State Guard is part of the Texas Military Department

The Texas Military Department includes the Texas Army National Guard, Texas Air National Guard and Texas State Guard.

## 2. The governor is commander in chief at the state level

The Texas State Guard serves under state authority rather than functioning as a federal military branch.

## 3. Its modern mission centers on state support

The current mission includes supporting state and local authorities during emergencies, homeland-security/community-service activities under Defense Support to Civil Authorities and augmenting Texas National Guard forces when required.

## 4. Texas militia history predates the modern organization

Texas had militia and volunteer military traditions long before the modern State Guard structure. Historical lineage should be described carefully so institutional continuity is not overstated.

## 5. World War II reshaped state-guard authority

When National Guard units entered federal service, federal law authorized states to create defense forces for home-state missions.

## 6. The Texas Defense Guard formed in 1941

Texas enacted the Texas Defense Guard Act in 1941 during the World War II mobilization period.

## 7. The organization later became the Texas State Guard

Names, structure and missions evolved after World War II. Dates and organizational transitions should use Texas Military Department records.

## 8. Disaster response is a major modern role

Texas State Guard personnel have supported major weather disasters, sheltering/logistics missions and other state emergencies.

## 9. Hurricane Harvey is part of its recent public-service history

Texas Military Department materials cite support to people displaced by Hurricane Harvey among the organization’s modern response examples.

## 10. The Columbia disaster response is another major example

The State Guard supported response activity after the Space Shuttle Columbia disaster.

## 11. The State Guard can support civil authorities without being ordinary law enforcement

Mission descriptions should not casually imply police powers or authority outside actual state orders and law.

## 12. Membership is not the same as federal active-duty military service

Benefits, status, activation and legal authority differ from federal armed forces and National Guard federal service. Current recruiting/benefit details require direct TMD verification.

## A useful timeline

- 19th century: earlier Texas militia/state-force history;
- 1940: federal State Guard Amendment framework;
- 1941: Texas Defense Guard organized during National Guard federalization;
- postwar decades: state-force reorganizations;
- modern era: disaster response, support missions and civil-authority assistance.
