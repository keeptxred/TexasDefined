# Classic Outdoor Games Texas Kids Still Play

Most playground games are not uniquely Texan. The Texas angle should come from climate, yards, parks, school traditions and regional variations rather than falsely claiming ownership of common games.

## Games and activities

- Tag and freeze tag
- Hide-and-seek
- Red Rover where schools/groups still allow it safely
- Kickball
- Wiffle ball or backyard baseball
- Touch football
- Basketball in driveways and parks
- Four square
- Hopscotch
- Jump rope
- Capture the flag
- Water-balloon or sprinkler games in hot weather
- Horseshoes or washers with adult supervision
- Sidewalk chalk games
- Bike and scooter circuits
- Nature scavenger hunts

## Texas-specific practical note

Outdoor play should be adjusted for extreme heat, poor air quality, lightning, mosquitoes, fire ants and local park conditions. Midday summer heat can be unsafe.

## Internal-link plan

Link to heat safety, nature centers, family parks, conservation education and school/family guides.

## Image guidance

Use one rights-cleared playground or empty game setup; avoid identifiable children without permission.
