# Things You’ll See at a Texas Tailgate Party

Tailgating differs by school, stadium, sport and fan group. This should capture common scenes without presenting alcohol or extreme behavior as required for participation.

## Familiar tailgate elements

- Grills, smokers or prepared barbecue
- Breakfast tacos at early starts
- School/team colors on tents, coolers and table setups
- Generators, fans or shade planning during hot games
- TVs or radios carrying other games
- Lawn games where venue rules allow them
- Family groups spanning several generations
- Alumni organizations and student groups
- Rival-fan banter that stays good-natured
- Boots, jerseys, polos and school-color outfits in the same parking lot
- Weather contingency plans
- Reusable water and hydration setups
- Campus or stadium traditions specific to the host

## Safety and venue rules

Current tailgating zones, grills, generators, glass, open flames, alcohol and parking rules must come from the venue or university. Never normalize drunk driving; include designated-driver/transit options where relevant.

## Internal-link plan

Link to college rivalries, stadiums, football culture, barbecue and sports-travel pages.

## Image guidance

Use one rights-cleared wide tailgate scene; avoid identifiable minors and copyrighted broadcast screens/logos as the focal subject.
