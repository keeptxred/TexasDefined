# Old-School Traits Associated With a Texas Gentleman

“Texas gentleman” is a cultural archetype, not a rulebook for masculinity. The useful version keeps the positive etiquette ideas while dropping gender policing and nostalgia for unequal social systems.

## Traits worth keeping from the archetype

- Keeping your word
- Showing up when you said you would
- Treating service workers with respect
- Offering practical help without making a performance of it
- Making guests feel welcome
- Respecting elders without assuming age makes someone automatically right
- Knowing how to disagree without threatening or humiliating people
- Caring for animals, land and tools responsibly
- Dressing appropriately for the occasion rather than showing off
- Sending thanks after genuine hospitality
- Making room for people with different backgrounds
- Taking responsibility for mistakes
- Learning local history instead of only repeating myths
- Being generous without keeping score
- Understanding that courtesy is not weakness

## Editorial note

Frame these as broadly useful character traits historically associated with the archetype—not obligations for men or proof of being “Texan enough.”
