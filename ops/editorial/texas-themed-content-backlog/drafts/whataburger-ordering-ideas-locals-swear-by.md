# Whataburger Ordering Ideas Texans Talk About

Whataburger customization is part of the chain’s appeal, but “secret menu hacks” often mix current options with outdated social-media claims. A responsible guide should use only combinations supported by the **current official menu/customization system**.

## 1. Customize the standard burger instead of chasing a secret item

Start with a currently listed burger and use official topping, cheese and sauce options.

## 2. Add jalapeños where offered

Jalapeños are a straightforward Texas-friendly customization, but availability and upcharges should be checked in the current menu/app.

## 3. Compare cheese choices

Where multiple cheeses are offered, the guide can explain flavor combinations without claiming every location stocks every option.

## 4. Use official sauces intentionally

Whataburger’s sauce selection changes over time. Current sauce names should be refreshed before promotion.

## 5. Breakfast-on-a-bun customization

Breakfast items may support meat, egg, cheese and condiment changes. Exact breakfast hours and item configuration are annual-review fields.

## 6. Taquito combinations

Taquitos are a durable part of Whataburger’s breakfast identity, but available fillings and breakfast windows require current confirmation.

## 7. Patty Melt customization

If the Patty Melt remains on the official menu, readers can use normal customization rather than invent a “hack.”

## 8. Spicy chicken modifications

Chicken sandwich availability and spice levels can change, so the final article should build from current official menu items only.

## 9. Bun and bread substitutions

Only recommend bread substitutions currently supported by the ordering interface or restaurant policy.

## 10. Vegetable-heavy burger customization

Extra lettuce, tomato, pickles, onions or other listed toppings can create a different texture/flavor without pretending it is an off-menu product.

## 11. Smaller portions through regular menu choices

A practical guide can compare junior/smaller menu options where currently offered rather than encouraging food waste.

## 12. Limited-time items belong in a current-year callout

Seasonal burgers, shakes and collaborations should never become evergreen body copy.

## What this article should not do

- invent staff-only codes;
- promise discontinued items;
- instruct employees to break policy;
- reproduce prices without date/location;
- make nutrition or allergen assumptions from old menus.
