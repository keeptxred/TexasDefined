# Signs You’ve Settled Into Life After Moving to Texas

The original “gone native” framing can imply there is one correct way to become Texan. A better version is about practical adaptation and familiarity after moving.

## Signs the place is starting to feel familiar

1. You plan errands around heat, traffic and thunderstorms.
2. You know which H-E-B, Kroger, local market or neighborhood grocery works best for you.
3. You understand that a two-hour drive can still be considered a reasonable day trip.
4. You have learned the difference between Texas regions instead of talking about the state as one landscape.
5. You have a preferred barbecue style or at least know why brisket, sausage and East Texas chopped beef create different conversations.
6. You check property-tax and school-district boundaries before assuming two nearby houses have the same costs.
7. You know ERCOT, your transmission utility and your retail electric provider are not necessarily the same entity.
8. You keep a weather-alert plan for severe storms and extreme heat.
9. You have learned when bluebonnet traffic peaks and how to avoid damaging roadside flowers.
10. You understand that “county,” “city” and “ETJ” boundaries can affect services and rules.
11. You have found a favorite small-town stop, park, trail or local restaurant that is not a headline attraction.
12. You have stopped treating every Texas stereotype as a statewide truth.
