# Texas Fashion Trends Through the Decades

Texas style has never been just cowboy boots. Ranch clothing, Mexican and border traditions, oil-boom wealth, rodeo fashion, music scenes, suburban retail and designer western wear have all shaped what Texans wear.

## 1. Ranch utility before “western fashion”

Boots, hats, denim and durable workwear began as practical equipment long before they became fashion symbols.

## 2. Vaquero influence

Texas cowboy clothing owes major debts to Mexican vaquero traditions, including riding gear, hat forms, leatherwork and decorative elements.

## 3. Early-20th-century western shirts

Snap shirts, yokes, embroidery and rodeo/show clothing helped turn ranch-derived clothing into a recognizable public style.

## 4. Oil-boom tailoring and city style

Houston, Dallas and Fort Worth developed wealthy urban fashion cultures that looked very different from ranch wear.

## 5. Western swing stagewear

Musicians helped popularize flashy western suits and coordinated band clothing, turning regional music into visual culture.

## 6. Postwar denim

Jeans moved from workwear toward mainstream youth and casual fashion, while remaining functional ranch clothing.

## 7. 1960s–70s Austin counterculture

Austin’s music and university scenes mixed denim, boots, folk influence, vintage western wear and countercultural style.

## 8. Rhinestone and rodeo glamour

Rodeo and country performers made sparkle, embroidery, fringe and highly shaped hats part of Texas visual identity.

## 9. Urban cowboy era

The late-1970s/early-1980s country boom brought boots, western shirts and dance-hall fashion into mainstream American retail.

## 10. Dallas power dressing

Corporate growth and popular television reinforced a polished, luxury image of Texas city fashion during the 1980s.

## 11. Tejano fashion

Tejano performers brought distinctive stagewear, boots, belts, tailored western silhouettes and Mexican American style into statewide and national visibility.

## 12. 1990s country casual

Denim, boots, oversized shirts and mainstream country styling spread far beyond rural Texas.

## 13. Modern western luxury

Contemporary Texas brands and designers combine high-end materials with western silhouettes, custom boots and bespoke hats.

## 14. Vintage western revival

Young shoppers increasingly mix old belt buckles, pearl snaps, boots and thrifted western pieces with contemporary fashion.

## 15. Texas style remains regional

Fort Worth western wear, Austin vintage/creative style, Houston global fashion, Dallas luxury retail, border-city traditions and ranch communities should not be flattened into one dress code.
