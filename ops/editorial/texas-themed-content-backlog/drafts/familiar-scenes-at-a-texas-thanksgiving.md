# Familiar Scenes at a Texas Thanksgiving

Texas Thanksgiving tables vary by family and community. This list should celebrate the mix instead of presenting one menu as authentic.

## Familiar scenes

- Turkey sharing the table with smoked meat in some families
- Tamales arriving from a family order or homemade batch
- Cornbread dressing and family-specific arguments about ingredients
- Pecan pie alongside other regional desserts
- Football on televisions in multiple rooms
- Outdoor seating when the weather cooperates
- A sudden cold front changing the whole plan
- Long drives across the state for family gatherings
- Leftover containers being assigned before dessert ends
- Recipes carrying Czech, German, Mexican, Southern, Black, Cajun/Creole or other family histories
- Kids moving between indoor food and outdoor games
- A relative arriving with a dish everyone expected but nobody formally assigned
- Friday plans involving state parks, shopping, football or simply leftovers
- Family photos in the same spot as previous years
- The meal functioning as a family-history archive as much as dinner
