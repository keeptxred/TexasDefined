# Ways Texans Use Jalapeños in Everyday Cooking

Jalapeños are common across Tex-Mex, barbecue-adjacent and home cooking, but heat varies by pepper and preparation.

## Everyday uses

1. Fresh slices on tacos.
2. Pickled jalapeños on nachos.
3. Diced into queso.
4. Roasted into salsa.
5. Mixed into cornbread.
6. Added to breakfast tacos.
7. Stuffed and grilled.
8. Chopped into pimento-cheese-style spreads.
9. Added to burger toppings.
10. Blended into creamy sauces.
11. Used in beans.
12. Added to slaw or relish.
13. Paired with fruit in salsa.
14. Mixed into deviled eggs.
15. Used sparingly in marinades after recipe testing.

## Handling note

Capsaicin can irritate skin and eyes. Wash hands and tools well after cutting peppers, and consider gloves when handling large quantities. Pepper heat is inconsistent, so taste cautiously.
