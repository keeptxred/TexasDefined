# Iconic Western-Wear Stores in Texas — With Craft and Fit in Mind

Western-wear shopping in Texas ranges from huge multi-brand retailers to bootmakers, hat shops and family stores that have served ranch communities for decades. A good guide should compare **what each store is actually good for** rather than rank them on fame alone.

## Store types to include

### Fort Worth Stockyards stores
The Stockyards contain western-wear shops serving tourists, working customers and event visitors. Current store roster and ownership require verification.

### Cavender’s
A Texas-founded western retailer with locations across the state and beyond. The article should distinguish chain convenience from custom craft.

### Pinto Ranch
A higher-end western retail concept with Texas locations/history. Current stores and brands require annual verification.

### Allen’s Boots — Austin
A South Congress landmark associated with large boot selections and Austin tourism. Current operation/location must be verified before promotion.

### M.L. Leddy’s — Fort Worth/San Angelo history
Leddy’s connects custom bootmaking and western retail to long Texas craft history. Current locations/services require direct verification.

### Custom hat shops
Texas has multiple hatters that shape or make felt/straw hats. A store should be included only when the current service—retail, shaping, custom build—is verified.

## What shoppers should compare

- work versus fashion use;
- boot fit and construction;
- hat material and shaping;
- repair services;
- custom versus factory-made;
- return policy;
- whether products are actually made in Texas;
- price range.

## “Western” does not automatically mean Texas-made

Many excellent western products are manufactured elsewhere. The article should label origin accurately rather than turn a Texas retail location into a made-in-Texas claim.
