# Classic Foods You May See at Texas Holiday Parties

Texas holiday food reflects many communities and family traditions. This list should showcase the mix rather than define one official menu.

## Foods and traditions to include

- Tamales
- Queso and tortilla chips
- Smoked brisket, turkey or ham
- Sausage reflecting Central Texas meat-market traditions
- Cornbread dressing
- Pecan pie
- Sheet cake or other family desserts
- Kolaches/klobasniky and Czech-Texas baking traditions where relevant
- German-Texas cookies and baked goods
- Gulf seafood in coastal families
- Deviled eggs
- Cheese balls and party dips
- Chili for casual gatherings
- Breakfast tacos after late-night celebrations
- Homemade candy, pralines or pecan treats

## Food-safety note

Hot and cold party foods need safe holding temperatures. Any detailed safety instructions should use current USDA/AgriLife guidance.
