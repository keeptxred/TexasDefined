# Signs You’re a Texan Living Out of State

This should be playful nostalgia, not a claim that people who leave Texas never adapt elsewhere.

## Familiar situations

1. You compare every long drive to an in-state Texas drive.
2. You notice immediately when barbecue vocabulary means something different.
3. You still track Texas weather because family or friends are there.
4. You explain that Texas has forests, mountains and coast—not only ranch country.
5. You look for familiar grocery or restaurant products when visiting home.
6. You know exactly which Texas foods travel badly and which freeze well.
7. You keep a Texas sports allegiance even when local teams are more convenient.
8. You correct the pronunciation of a Texas town without thinking about it.
9. A trip home becomes a list of food, family and place-based stops.
10. You recognize Texas plates from farther away than seems reasonable.
11. You compare local property taxes, energy systems or weather with Texas out of habit.
12. You miss one specific landscape—Gulf Coast, Hill Country, Panhandle, East Texas or West Texas—more than “Texas” in the abstract.
13. You still call some interstate highways by their Texas shorthand.
14. You own at least one Texas-themed object that became more meaningful after leaving.
15. You can appreciate the new state while still feeling attached to Texas.
