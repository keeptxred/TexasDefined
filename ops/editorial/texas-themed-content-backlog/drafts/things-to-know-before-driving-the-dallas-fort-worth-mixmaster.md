# Things to Know Before Driving the Dallas–Fort Worth Mixmaster

“Mixmaster” is local shorthand applied to major freeway interchanges in the DFW area. Because highway geometry and construction change, this should focus on driving habits rather than fixed lane instructions.

## Practical habits

1. Set navigation before moving.
2. Review the route overview, not only the next turn.
3. Know whether you need I-35E or I-35W well before the split.
4. Expect lane assignments to change around construction.
5. Do not make a dangerous last-second multi-lane move to save a missed exit.
6. Leave more following distance in heavy traffic.
7. Watch for merging traffic from both sides where signs indicate it.
8. Keep right except when passing where Texas law requires.
9. Slow down or move over for stopped emergency/service vehicles as required by current Texas law.
10. Treat rain, ice and severe storms as reasons to add time or delay travel.
11. Use passenger help for complex navigation when available.
12. If you miss the exit, let navigation reroute you.
13. Check TxDOT traffic/closure information before major trips.
14. Avoid handling the phone while driving.
15. Remember that construction-specific advice can become obsolete quickly.
