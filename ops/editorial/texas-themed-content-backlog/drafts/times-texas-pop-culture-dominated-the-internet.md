# Times Texas Pop Culture Took Over the Internet

Internet culture moves quickly, so this should be a flexible social-history list rather than a claim that one meme “broke the internet.”

## Types of moments to include

- Texas weather events that spawned widespread original memes
- Viral sports celebrations or crowd traditions
- State Fair foods that spread far beyond Dallas feeds
- Buc-ee’s, Whataburger or H-E-B moments that became national conversations
- Texas music performances that generated major online reactions
- TV/film moments tied to Texas settings
- Bluebonnet-season photo culture
- Viral wildlife encounters handled with accurate species context
- Texas city slogans or signs that became reaction images
- Space/NASA moments tied to Houston
- Rodeo or livestock moments that crossed into mainstream social feeds
- Original Texas creators whose work reached national audiences

## Copyright and verification rules

Do not repost somebody else’s meme, TikTok, tweet, photo or video without permission/embeddable rights. Describe the phenomenon and link to an authorized original source when needed. Verify that a “viral Texas moment” actually originated in or materially involved Texas.

## Internal-link plan

Link each moment to deeper pages on weather, State Fair, brands, music, cities, sports, wildlife or NASA/space history.

## Image guidance

Create original graphics or use rights-cleared source imagery. No screenshot collages of social posts.
