# Things Texans Look Forward to Every Autumn

Texas autumn is less about one universal leaf-peeping season and more about cooler nights, football, fairs, festivals and the return of comfortable outdoor weather.

## Familiar fall markers

- The first meaningful cold front
- State Fair season in Dallas
- High-school and college football
- County fairs and harvest festivals
- Cooler camping weekends
- Hill Country and other regional fall color when conditions cooperate
- Patio weather returning after summer heat
- Hunting seasons for those who participate legally
- Fall migration and birding
- Pumpkin patches and farm events
- Pecan harvest and seasonal food
- Oktoberfest and German-Texas heritage events
- Rodeos and livestock events
- Less punishing hiking conditions
- Holiday planning beginning before the weather feels wintry

## Internal-link plan

Link to fall color, State Fair, football, county fairs, camping, birding, pecans and seasonal events.

## Verification notes

Current foliage, event, hunting and weather conditions require annual refreshes.
