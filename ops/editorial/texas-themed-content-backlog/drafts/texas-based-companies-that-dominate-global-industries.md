# Texas-Based Companies With Global Reach

Texas has become one of the country’s largest headquarters states, but a list of “companies that dominate” can become stale quickly when headquarters move, mergers happen or rankings change.

The durable version should explain **which industries Texas anchors** and use current-year examples only after headquarters verification.

## 1. Energy

Texas remains central to global oil, gas, refining, pipelines, electricity and energy services. Major corporate headquarters and operational centers span Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth and other metros.

## 2. Semiconductors and electronics

Texas Instruments is one of the clearest examples of a Texas-headquartered company with global semiconductor reach. Current headquarters and revenue/ranking claims require annual verification.

## 3. Airlines

American Airlines is headquartered in North Texas and connects DFW to a global network. Southwest Airlines is headquartered in Dallas.

## 4. Technology and computing

Dell Technologies grew from Austin and remains one of the state’s best-known global technology companies.

## 5. Telecommunications

AT&T’s Dallas headquarters ties Texas to a major global communications company.

## 6. Logistics and parcel delivery

Texas’ central location, airports, highways, rail and ports support major logistics operations and headquarters activity.

## 7. Chemicals and materials

Houston and Gulf Coast industrial corridors host companies with global chemical, plastics and industrial-material footprints.

## 8. Engineering and construction

Texas supports large engineering, infrastructure and project-services firms serving energy, transportation, aerospace and industrial markets.

## 9. Financial services

Dallas–Fort Worth has expanded as a financial-services and corporate-operations hub, while Austin and Houston also support major banking and investment activity.

## 10. Aerospace and space

Texas hosts NASA Johnson Space Center plus major aerospace and commercial-space operations. This is an industry cluster, not a claim that every leading space company is Texas-headquartered.

## 11. Retail

Texas-based retail and food-service companies can have national footprints even when they remain strongly associated with the state.

## 12. Automotive and advanced manufacturing

Vehicle and battery manufacturing investments have expanded in Texas, but plant announcements and production status must be verified before promotion.

## Why headquarters counts change

The Office of the Governor reported **57 Fortune 500 headquarters in Texas on the 2026 Fortune 500 list**, based on 2025 fiscal-year revenue. That number should be date-labeled and refreshed annually rather than embedded as a timeless fact.

## A better way to compare Texas companies

Instead of ranking “most dominant,” classify by:

- global revenue/market reach;
- headquarters versus major operational presence;
- sector leadership;
- Texas employment;
- export role;
- historical impact;
- current public-company status.
