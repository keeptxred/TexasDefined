# Famous Battles Fought on Texas Soil

Texas battle history spans Spanish colonial conflicts, the Texas Revolution, the Civil War, Indigenous resistance and border warfare. A useful guide should explain causes and consequences rather than reduce every site to battlefield tourism.

## Battles and campaigns to research

### Battle of the Alamo
Place the 1836 siege within the wider Texas Revolution, including the political and military situation before and after the battle.

### Goliad-era fighting
Separate the Battle of Coleto and the later Goliad executions from the broader campaign.

### Battle of San Jacinto
Explain why the battle was decisive and how it led to the capture of Santa Anna and the Treaties of Velasco.

### Battle of Palmito Ranch
Near Brownsville, this May 1865 engagement is commonly described as the last land battle of the Civil War; phrase the claim precisely and source it.

### Adobe Walls and Red River War context
Indigenous-settler/U.S. military conflict needs tribal perspectives and should not be framed as empty frontier adventure.

## Visiting battle sites responsibly

Use official parks, museums, markers and public trails. Some landscapes are private or have changed dramatically since the conflict.

## Verification notes

Use NPS, THC, TSLAC, TSHA, battlefield parks and tribal/nation sources. Be precise about battle dates, participants, casualty estimates and disputed interpretations.

## Image guidance

Use rights-cleared battlefield landscapes, maps or archival art with clear provenance; avoid presenting later romantic paintings as eyewitness evidence.
