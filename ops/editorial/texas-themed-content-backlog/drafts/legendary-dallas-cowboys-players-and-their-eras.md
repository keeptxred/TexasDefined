# Legendary Dallas Cowboys Players and Their Eras

The Dallas Cowboys’ history is easiest to understand by era rather than by pretending there is an objective all-time ranking.

## Landry-era foundations

### Bob Lilly
A foundational defensive star whose career spans the franchise’s rise from expansion team to contender.

### Roger Staubach
A defining quarterback of the 1970s whose career connects Navy service, late NFL entry and championship-era Cowboys football.

### Tony Dorsett and Randy White
Both help explain how the team transitioned through the late Landry years.

## The 1990s dynasty

### Troy Aikman
The quarterback of three Super Bowl championship teams in the decade.

### Emmitt Smith
The running back became the NFL’s career rushing leader and central figure in the offense.

### Michael Irvin
A key receiver whose career also illustrates the personality-heavy national profile of the dynasty.

## Later franchise eras

Players such as Jason Witten, DeMarcus Ware, Tony Romo and others represent important post-dynasty periods even without another championship.

## How to handle “legendary”

Use objective anchors—Hall of Fame induction, All-Pro selections, franchise records, championships and era impact—while acknowledging that fan rankings remain subjective.
