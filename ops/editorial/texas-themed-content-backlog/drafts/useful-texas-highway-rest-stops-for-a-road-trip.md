# Texas Highway Rest Stops That Are More Useful Than a Gas-Station Bathroom

Texas is large enough that a good rest stop can materially improve a road trip. TxDOT operates a statewide network of safety rest areas and Travel Information Centers with facilities that may include 24-hour restrooms, separate truck and passenger parking, picnic areas, Wi-Fi, walking paths, security and regional visitor information.

The useful angle is not a subjective ranking. It is knowing where a public stop can solve a practical travel problem.

## Start with the TxDOT network

TxDOT currently reports 76 Safety Rest Areas. Restrooms are open 24 hours a day, and most locations have attendants around the clock. Facilities vary by site.

That makes TxDOT’s current rest-area map the source of record; a TexasDefined article should never assume an old rest stop is still operating because it appears in a travel blog.

## Langtry Travel Information Center — US 90

Langtry is one of the most memorable state-operated stops because it combines traveler services with the Judge Roy Bean visitor complex.

Current 2026 conditions matter: TxDOT reports construction affecting parts of the historic visitor area, while lobby/restroom access remains available. Any description of the Jersey Lilly, Opera House or gardens needs a same-day/current check before publication.

## Culberson County Safety Rest Areas — I-10

West Texas rest areas can be especially valuable because service gaps are longer and summer heat is more consequential. Current TxDOT facility pages should be used for exact amenities and direction of travel.

## Ward County Safety Rest Areas — I-20

The Monahans/Ward County corridor gives travelers another state-maintained break across West Texas. This is a good place to explain why truck and passenger-car parking separation matters on freight-heavy routes.

## Bell County Safety Rest Areas — I-35

Along one of the state’s busiest corridors, Bell County rest areas can provide a public alternative to crowded commercial exits. Current direction, closures and amenities must be verified from TxDOT.

## Chambers County / East Texas I-10 corridor

Public rest stops east of Houston help long-distance travelers between the Houston region and Louisiana. The final guide should name only facilities confirmed on TxDOT’s current list and distinguish Safety Rest Areas from Travel Information Centers.

## Waskom Travel Information Center

Near the Louisiana line, Waskom functions as a Texas welcome point as well as a rest stop. Travel Information Centers can provide maps and regional planning help that a basic safety rest area may not.

## Orange Travel Information Center

Orange serves the eastern I-10 gateway along the Gulf Coast. It can link into Southeast Texas, Gulf Coast and Louisiana-border travel content.

## Laredo and Harlingen Travel Information Centers

South Texas centers are useful for travelers entering the state or moving through the border region and Rio Grande Valley. Current operating hours matter even though restrooms/services at some highway facilities may have different schedules.

## Amenities that actually matter

When comparing stops, TexasDefined should use transparent features rather than star ratings:

- 24-hour restrooms;
- separate truck/car parking;
- accessible restrooms and parking;
- staffed visitor information;
- picnic/shade areas;
- walking space;
- tornado/storm shelters where provided;
- security/attendants;
- pet-relief space;
- current construction/closure status.

## Rest areas are not overnight campgrounds

A final version should cite current TxDOT rules before giving advice about length of stay, sleeping in a vehicle or commercial activity. Do not extrapolate rules from another state.
