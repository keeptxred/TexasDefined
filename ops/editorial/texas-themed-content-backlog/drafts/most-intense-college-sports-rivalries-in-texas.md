# Texas College Sports Rivalries That Carry More Than a Scoreboard

Texas college rivalries are not all the same. Some are active annual conference games, some survived conference realignment, and some are historic series that now appear only intermittently.

A useful guide should tell readers **what the rivalry is, why it matters, and whether it is actually active now** rather than pretending every famous matchup still appears on the schedule every year.

## 1. Texas vs. Texas A&M — Lone Star Showdown

The Texas–Texas A&M football rivalry dates to 1894 and became one of the defining sports traditions in the state.

The series stopped after the 2011 season when conference realignment separated the schools. It returned in 2024 when Texas joined Texas A&M in the SEC. Texas A&M’s official athletics site described the 2024 game as the first meeting since 2011 and the 119th installment of the series.

That return changed this rivalry from nostalgia back into a current annual-storyline candidate. Any live version should confirm the newest SEC schedule before saying exactly when or where the next game will be played.

## 2. Texas vs. Oklahoma — Red River Rivalry

Texas–Oklahoma is unusual because one of the state’s defining rivalries is against a school across the Red River.

Texas Athletics says the series began in 1900, has been played annually in Dallas since 1912 and has been tied to the State Fair of Texas since 1929. The neutral-site Cotton Bowl setting makes the game part football rivalry, part Texas State Fair tradition and part Dallas civic ritual.

Because both programs now compete in the SEC, the rivalry survived a major conference transition rather than being broken by it.

## 3. Baylor vs. TCU

Baylor and TCU share deep private-school and regional history, and their football series has repeatedly produced high-stakes games.

The schools’ geographic proximity and long histories in Texas conferences make this a rivalry that can be explained through the evolution of college sports in the state rather than only one memorable season.

Before publication, verify the current series totals and latest matchup through Baylor and TCU athletics.

## 4. TCU vs. SMU — Battle for the Iron Skillet

The Iron Skillet links Fort Worth and Dallas through two private universities whose football histories have repeatedly crossed.

TCU’s official recap called the 2025 meeting the 104th rendition of the rivalry. Conference membership has changed, but the matchup continued at least through 2025.

Because future scheduling can change, this entry should be tagged with current-status language rather than guaranteeing indefinite annual play.

## 5. Texas A&M vs. LSU

Texas A&M–LSU combines older regional history with modern SEC stakes.

The matchup gained new relevance after Texas A&M joined the SEC, and it now sits within a broader Gulf Coast recruiting and conference context.

Do not describe it as older or more historically central than Texas–Texas A&M; instead explain why it became more important in the SEC era.

## 6. Texas vs. Arkansas

Texas–Arkansas helped define parts of the old Southwest Conference era.

The series no longer carries the same uninterrupted annual rhythm it once did, but conference realignment has again put both schools in the SEC orbit.

This is a good example of a rivalry whose historical importance can exceed its recent frequency.

## 7. Texas Tech vs. Baylor

Texas Tech and Baylor share a long history of in-state conference competition.

The rivalry can be used to explain how Big 12 membership preserved some Texas matchups even as Texas and Texas A&M moved into the SEC.

Current series status and future schedules should be verified through school or Big 12 sources before promotion.

## 8. Prairie View A&M vs. Texas Southern

Texas college-sports history is incomplete if it focuses only on the largest FBS programs.

Prairie View A&M and Texas Southern represent two major Texas HBCU athletic traditions. Their rivalry and Houston-area showcase games connect college football to Black educational history, marching-band culture and alumni tradition.

Use official school/SWAC sources for current game branding and schedule details.

## 9. Sam Houston vs. Stephen F. Austin

The Piney Woods rivalry between Sam Houston and Stephen F. Austin has deep East Texas roots.

Conference moves have affected scheduling, so publication copy should distinguish the historical rivalry from its current frequency.

This entry is especially useful for connecting college sports to Huntsville, Nacogdoches and East Texas place pages.

## 10. Rivalries change when conferences change

The modern Texas college-sports map is dramatically different from the Southwest Conference era.

A strong article should include a status label for every rivalry:

- **active annual or regular series**;
- **active but schedule-dependent**;
- **historic/intermittent**.

That one editorial device prevents the article from misleading readers after future conference or scheduling changes.

## Why these games matter beyond football

Rivalry weekends affect:

- alumni travel;
- campus traditions;
- marching bands;
- State Fair attendance;
- recruiting;
- local hotels and restaurants;
- family traditions spanning generations.

That makes this article a natural bridge among Sports, Events, university towns, road trips and Texas history.
