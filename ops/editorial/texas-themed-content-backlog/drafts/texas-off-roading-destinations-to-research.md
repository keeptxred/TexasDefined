# Texas Off-Roading Destinations to Research

Texas off-roading ranges from privately managed OHV parks to designated public routes. The key distinction is legal access: a scenic dirt road, ranch track or dry creek bed is not automatically open to recreational vehicles.

## What to verify before loading the trailer

- The property currently allows your vehicle class.
- An OHV decal, permit, waiver or reservation is required or not.
- Trails match your vehicle, tires and recovery equipment.
- Fire/weather closures are not in effect.
- Camping, night riding and alcohol rules are understood.
- Street-legal requirements apply if the route uses public roads.

## Types of destinations

### TPWD OHV program sites
Use current TPWD OHV program information to identify participating locations and legal requirements.

### Private OHV parks
Private parks can provide mapped trails, recovery support and camping, but operating status and vehicle rules must be checked directly.

### West Texas and desert routes
Remote terrain can create heat, fuel, tire and communications risks far beyond ordinary trail driving.

### East Texas forest routes
Forest roads and motorized-trail systems can have seasonal closures and route restrictions designed to protect soils and habitat.

## Leave No Trace for motorized recreation

Stay on legal routes, avoid wet trails when rutting would damage them, pack out trash, respect noise rules and never bypass a closed gate.

## Recovery planning

Carry water, navigation, a first-aid kit, tire repair/inflation equipment and recovery gear appropriate to the vehicle. Remote travel should include a communications and check-in plan.

## Verification notes

Refresh from TPWD, USFS/BLM/NPS where applicable, county road authorities and the property/operator itself. Never describe unauthorized riverbeds, utility easements, ranch roads or closed public lands as riding destinations.

## Image guidance

Use one rights-cleared image showing legal trail use with safe vehicle operation and minimal environmental damage.
