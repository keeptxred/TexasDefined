# Austin Live-Music Venues Worth Knowing

Austin’s music identity is built on venues of very different sizes and histories. The useful guide is not a permanent “top ten” ranking; it is a map of the city’s musical ecosystems with current operating status verified before publication.

## 1. Antone’s Nightclub

Founded in 1975, Antone’s is central to Austin blues history and remains active on East Fifth Street. Current calendar and age restrictions require direct venue verification.

## 2. Continental Club

The South Congress venue dates to 1955 and remains closely associated with roots, rockabilly, country and Austin’s club tradition.

## 3. ACL Live at the Moody Theater

ACL Live is the permanent home of the *Austin City Limits* television program and a major downtown concert venue.

## 4. Stubb’s Waller Creek Amphitheater

Stubb’s combines live music with a barbecue/restaurant identity. Current operator, calendar and event access should be checked directly.

## 5. Mohawk

A Red River District venue known for indie, rock and touring acts. Annual verification should cover operating status and event policies.

## 6. The White Horse

East Austin’s dance-hall/bar setting is associated with country, honky-tonk and dancing culture.

## 7. Saxon Pub

A smaller South Austin listening-room tradition that fits singer-songwriter and roots audiences.

## 8. C-Boy’s Heart & Soul

A South Congress-area club connected to soul, blues, rock and Austin nightlife.

## 9. Broken Spoke

The Broken Spoke is a historic dance-hall/country venue whose cultural role extends beyond a standard concert room.

## 10. Paramount Theatre

The historic downtown theater hosts music, comedy, film and stage events and links Austin entertainment to architectural preservation.

## Additional venue types to cover

Austin’s scene also includes:

- small Red River clubs;
- outdoor amphitheaters;
- hotel/listening rooms;
- university venues;
- dance halls outside the urban core;
- large arena shows.

## How to choose a venue

Ask what experience you want:

- seated listening room;
- dance floor;
- blues history;
- local residency;
- touring indie show;
- outdoor concert;
- major national act;
- historic theater.
