# Things Texans May Miss Most When Traveling Abroad

Travel should broaden perspective, not become a contest between Texas and the destination. This list can capture homesickness while staying respectful of other countries.

## Things someone might miss

- Family and familiar routines
- A particular style of Tex-Mex or barbecue
- Free refills and very large iced drinks
- Familiar highway driving and parking expectations
- Air conditioning standards during hot weather
- Specific grocery products or hot sauces
- College or professional sports broadcasts on the usual schedule
- Familiar English/Spanish Texas speech patterns
- Wide-open landscapes and long horizons
- A favorite breakfast taco
- Familiar weather alerts and local news
- A dog-friendly backyard or porch routine
- Texas craft beer, soda or snack brands
- The ease of knowing exactly how tipping, taxes and service work at home
- The people more than any product

## Editorial note

Avoid portraying foreign customs as inferior because they differ from U.S./Texas norms. The joke should be about homesickness and habit.
