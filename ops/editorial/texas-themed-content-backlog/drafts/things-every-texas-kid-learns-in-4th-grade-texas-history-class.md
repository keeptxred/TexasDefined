# Texas History Topics Many Students Encounter in Fourth Grade

Texas curriculum standards change, so this should never claim every classroom teaches the same lesson in the same way. The article should use current TEKS as the authority.

- Texas regions and geography
- Indigenous peoples and cultures
- Spanish exploration and missions
- Mexican Texas and settlement
- The Texas Revolution
- The Republic of Texas
- Annexation and statehood
- The Civil War and Reconstruction in Texas
- Cattle, ranching and railroads
- Oil and industrial growth
- Immigration and cultural diversity
- Important civic institutions and state symbols
- Major historical figures examined in context
- Economic activities and natural resources
- How local communities connect to state history
