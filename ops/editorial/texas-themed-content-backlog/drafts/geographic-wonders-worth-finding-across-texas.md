# Geographic Wonders Worth Finding Across Texas

Texas spans deserts, mountains, prairie, forest, barrier islands, canyonlands, spring systems and major river basins. This guide should be a geographic overview, not another generic attraction list.

## Wonders by landscape type

### Mountain and desert country
Guadalupe Mountains, Chihuahuan Desert landscapes and Big Bend reveal some of the state’s strongest elevation and geologic contrasts.

### Canyons and escarpments
Palo Duro Canyon and the Caprock Escarpment help explain the transition between the High Plains and lower country.

### Karst, springs and caves
Central Texas limestone supports caves, sinkholes and major springs where groundwater and surface landscapes are tightly connected.

### Piney Woods and wetlands
East Texas forests, bottomlands and bayous show a completely different ecological and hydrologic Texas.

### Barrier islands and coastal systems
Padre Island and the broader Gulf barrier coast are dynamic systems shaped by waves, storms, sediment and wind.

### Rivers and reservoirs
The Rio Grande, Brazos, Colorado, Guadalupe and other rivers organize settlement, recreation and water planning across regions.

## Read the landscape, not just the attraction

For each destination, explain the larger landform, how it formed, what ecosystem it supports and which public agency manages access.
