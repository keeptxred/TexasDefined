# Moments That Define Houston Astros History

Houston’s Major League Baseball history stretches from the Colt .45s and the Astrodome through National League pennants, an American League dynasty, two World Series championships and a sign-stealing scandal that permanently affects how the 2017 title must be discussed.

A credible history page has to include both the celebrations and the controversy.

## 1. The Colt .45s begin play in 1962

Houston entered the National League as the Colt .45s in 1962.

That expansion season established Major League Baseball in Houston before the franchise took the Astros name three years later.

## 2. The Astros name and the Astrodome era begin in 1965

In 1965 the club became the Houston Astros and began playing in the Astrodome, tying its new identity to Houston’s space-age image.

The move helped make indoor baseball and Houston’s futuristic civic branding part of the same story.

## 3. Nolan Ryan’s Houston years

Ryan brought historic strikeout power and national attention to the Astros.

His Houston tenure belongs in the article as part of the franchise’s long pre-championship era, not simply as a bridge to his later Rangers career.

## 4. The 1980 postseason breakthrough

The Astros reached the National League Championship Series in 1980, one of the franchise’s first deep postseason runs.

This period showed that Houston could become a serious October baseball city decades before its championship era.

## 5. The Killer B’s era

Craig Biggio and Jeff Bagwell became the faces of a generation of winning Astros teams.

Biggio’s 3,000th hit in 2007 and both players’ eventual Hall of Fame recognition make the era larger than a single postseason result.

## 6. The first World Series appearance in 2005

Houston defeated St. Louis in the NLCS to reach the World Series for the first time.

The White Sox swept the Astros, but the pennant ended decades of waiting and remains one of the central achievements of the National League era.

## 7. Moving from the National League to the American League

The Astros played their final National League season in 2012 and moved to the American League in 2013.

That realignment eventually created a regular in-state division rivalry with the Texas Rangers and fundamentally changed Houston’s competitive map.

## 8. The rebuild turns into contention in 2015

After years of heavy losses, the Astros returned to the postseason in 2015 under a young core that included José Altuve and Carlos Correa.

That season is the beginning of the modern competitive era.

## 9. The 2017 World Series championship

Houston defeated the Los Angeles Dodgers in seven games to win the franchise’s first World Series title.

The championship carried enormous emotional weight after Hurricane Harvey devastated the Houston region earlier that year.

But no responsible history page should stop there.

## 10. The 2017 sign-stealing findings

In January 2020, Major League Baseball released findings that the Astros used an illegal electronic sign-stealing system during the 2017 regular season and postseason. MLB imposed penalties, and the club dismissed general manager Jeff Luhnow and manager A.J. Hinch after their suspensions.

That finding is material context for the 2017 championship. TexasDefined should neither erase the title from the record nor present it without acknowledging MLB’s investigation and discipline.

## 11. José Altuve’s 2019 ALCS walk-off

Altuve’s walk-off home run against the Yankees sent Houston to the 2019 World Series.

The Astros ultimately lost that World Series to Washington, but the moment became one of the defining on-field images of the era.

## 12. Dusty Baker leads Houston back to a title

The Astros returned to the World Series in 2021 and lost to Atlanta, then came back in 2022.

Houston’s continued success after the 2017 scandal made the later championship especially important to the franchise’s competitive legacy.

## 13. The 2022 World Series championship

The Astros defeated Philadelphia in six games in 2022 for the franchise’s second World Series title.

The club’s official timeline notes a dominant 11–2 postseason run following a 106-win regular season, with Jeremy Peña winning both ALCS and World Series MVP honors.

## 14. The first all-Texas postseason series in 2023

Houston faced the Rangers in the 2023 ALCS, the first MLB postseason series between the two Texas clubs.

All seven games were won by the road team, and Texas eliminated Houston before winning the World Series.

The series elevated the Silver Boot rivalry into postseason history.

## 15. The long postseason streak ends

The Astros’ official timeline records that Houston reached the postseason eight consecutive seasons before missing it in 2025.

That endpoint matters because it gives the modern run a defined arc while leaving room for the next era.

## How to write the Astros era responsibly

Avoid two opposite errors:

- pretending the 2017 scandal means nothing else in the era happened;
- pretending the scandal is a footnote too minor to affect historical interpretation.

The article should distinguish official results, MLB findings and editorial interpretation.
