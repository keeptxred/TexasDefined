# Iconic Sports Broadcasters From Texas

Texas sports broadcasting spans local radio voices, national network careers and team announcers who became part of the identity of a franchise or city. This article should define the Texas connection instead of casually labeling anyone who worked one game in the state as “from Texas.”

## What qualifies

A broadcaster can fit through birth, upbringing, education, a long Texas broadcasting career or a defining association with a Texas team. The article should state which connection applies.

## Broadcasting eras to cover

### Radio-first voices
Before regional sports networks and streaming, radio announcers often carried games across huge parts of Texas and built especially strong fan relationships.

### Team television eras
Local and regional television expanded the visibility of MLB, NBA, NHL, college and high-school sports.

### National network careers
Some Texas-linked broadcasters moved into national play-by-play, studio and championship-event roles.

### Spanish-language broadcasting
Texas sports media also includes important Spanish-language radio and television traditions that should not be treated as an afterthought.

## What to document

- Texas connection
- Teams/leagues covered
- Years or era of major work
- Signature role or milestone
- Awards/hall-of-fame recognition where applicable
- Whether the broadcaster is active, retired or deceased

## Source notes

Use team/league media guides, broadcasters’ halls of fame, university archives, National Sports Media Association and reputable career histories. Verify active roles before publication.

## Image guidance

Use rights-cleared booth, microphone, stadium or archival broadcaster imagery. Do not assume network publicity photos are reusable.
