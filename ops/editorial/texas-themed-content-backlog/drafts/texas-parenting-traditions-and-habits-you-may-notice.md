# Texas Parenting Traditions and Habits You May Notice

There is no single “Texas parenting style.” This list should focus on place-based habits families may develop because of climate, distances, schools and local traditions.

## Habits worth discussing

- Keeping extra water and weather supplies in the vehicle
- Planning outdoor activities around heat and storms
- Long drives for sports, family visits or school events
- Teaching children about severe-weather alerts
- Family trips to state parks, rivers or historic sites
- Strong participation in school, band, FFA, athletics or other local programs where relevant
- Passing down food and holiday traditions
- Teaching respect for ranch/farm animals and wildlife in rural communities
- Bluebonnet-photo etiquette and roadside safety
- Learning local history through museums and family stories
- Treating grandparents and extended family as part of the childcare/social network in many families
- Building routines around regional festivals, rodeos or community events

## Editorial note

Avoid claims about discipline, religion, politics, gender roles or family structure as uniquely Texan. Texas families are diverse.
