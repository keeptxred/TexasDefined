# Fictional Characters That Capture Different Sides of Texas

This is a pop-culture conversation starter, not a claim that fictional characters accurately represent all Texans.

## Angles to include

- Characters rooted in ranch or western traditions
- Small-town Texas characters
- Urban Dallas/Houston/Austin/San Antonio portrayals
- Tejano and Mexican American Texas stories
- East Texas, Panhandle, Gulf Coast and borderland settings
- Characters that challenge rather than reinforce cowboy stereotypes

## Editorial rules

1. Explain the work and setting without copying dialogue.
2. Distinguish a story set in Texas from one actually filmed or produced here.
3. Avoid ranking caricatures as “the most Texan.”
4. Include different regions, eras and communities.
5. Use brief plot context only; no major spoilers unless clearly labeled.

## Internal-link plan

Link to Texas film/TV, regional culture, city pages and literature coverage.

## Image guidance

Do not use copyrighted film/TV stills without permission. Prefer a rights-cleared Texas setting connected to the work.
