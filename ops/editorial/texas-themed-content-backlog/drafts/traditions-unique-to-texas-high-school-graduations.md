# Texas High-School Graduation Traditions You May Notice

Graduation traditions vary by district, school and community. This should showcase examples without claiming they are statewide requirements.

## Traditions to explore

- Ceremonies in large football stadiums
- School-color cords, stoles or honor regalia
- Senior walks through elementary schools
- Community scholarship nights
- Baccalaureate services where privately/community organized and appropriate
- Class photos at local landmarks
- Decorated graduation caps where school rules allow them
- Family gatherings built around barbecue or Tex-Mex
- School songs, alma maters and fight songs
- Military-enlistment recognition in some communities
- CTE, dual-credit and academy recognitions
- Senior sunrise/sunset events
- Project Graduation-style supervised celebrations
- Local parade or downtown recognition in smaller towns
- Traditions tied to specific mascots, districts or communities

## Verification note

School ceremony rules, regalia and event names change. Verify current district/school information before naming a tradition as active.

## Internal-link plan

Link to school districts, mascots, college life, family guides and small-town community traditions.

## Image guidance

Use a rights-cleared empty stadium, cap/tassel or public graduation setup; avoid identifiable minors without clear permission.
