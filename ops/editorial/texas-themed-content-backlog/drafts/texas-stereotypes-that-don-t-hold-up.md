# Texas Stereotypes That Don’t Hold Up

Texas is too large, urban and culturally varied for many familiar stereotypes to survive contact with the real state.

## Stereotypes worth challenging

1. “Texas is all desert.” East Texas forests and the Gulf Coast quickly disprove that.
2. “Everyone owns a ranch.” Most Texans live in metropolitan areas.
3. “Everyone wears cowboy boots every day.” Western wear is important, but not universal.
4. “Texas has one accent.” Speech changes by region, generation and community.
5. “Texas food is only barbecue.” Tex-Mex, Gulf seafood, Czech/German traditions, Vietnamese Gulf Coast food and many other cuisines matter.
6. “Every Texan loves the same sports team.” Rivalries alone make that impossible.
7. “Small towns all look alike.” Settlement history and geography produce very different communities.
8. “Texas never gets cold.” Panhandle winters and statewide freezes say otherwise.
9. “The coast is just beaches.” Ports, wetlands, fishing, petrochemicals and barrier-island ecology all shape it.
10. “Texas culture is one thing.” It is layered and regional.
