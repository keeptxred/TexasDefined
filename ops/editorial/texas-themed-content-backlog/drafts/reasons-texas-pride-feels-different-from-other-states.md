# Reasons Texas Pride Feels Different From Other States

Texas pride is visible, marketable and sometimes exaggerated. A useful social piece should explain the historical and cultural reasons without claiming Texans care more about home than everyone else.

## Reasons the identity is unusually visible

1. Texas was an independent republic before statehood.
2. The Lone Star flag is simple and instantly recognizable.
3. The state’s size creates a strong sense of internal geography and regional travel.
4. Texas has nationally recognizable food, music and ranching traditions.
5. Major cities have distinct identities while still using the Texas label heavily.
6. State symbols appear constantly in architecture, brands and merchandise.
7. School history traditionally gives Texas its own concentrated curriculum.
8. Sports teams and universities reinforce regional/state identity.
9. Migration means many people actively choose Texas and then adopt pieces of its identity.
10. The state contains enough cultural difference that Texans can argue about what “Texas” means while still using the shared label.

## Editorial note

Do not turn pride into superiority. Include Indigenous, Tejano, Black, immigrant and modern metropolitan contributions to the identity.
