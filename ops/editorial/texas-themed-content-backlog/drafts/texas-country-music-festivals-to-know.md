# Texas Country-Music Festivals to Know

Texas has a dense network of country, western, Red Dirt, Americana and roots festivals. The useful version of this page should preserve the **festival identities and regions** while treating dates, lineups, venues, ticket policies and even operating status as annual-review fields.

## Festivals and event traditions to verify each year

### Larry Joe Taylor’s Texas Music Festival
A long-running Stephenville-area event associated with Texas/Red Dirt music culture.

### Two Step Inn — Georgetown
A newer large-scale Central Texas country festival. Annual lineup, dates and venue rules require current official confirmation.

### Old Settler’s Music Festival
A Central Texas roots/Americana institution whose programming overlaps country, bluegrass and related traditions.

### Kerrville Folk Festival
Not strictly a country festival, but foundational to Texas songwriting culture and useful in a broader country-roots guide.

### Brauntex / New Braunfels regional festival ecosystem
New Braunfels and Gruene host recurring country and roots events tied to dance-hall culture; exact festival brands should be verified annually.

### Fort Worth Stockyards music events
Country concerts and festival-style events recur in the Stockyards, but names and schedules change.

### Rodeo concert series
Houston, San Antonio, Austin and other rodeos combine livestock events with major country performances. These are annual event calendars, not evergreen lineups.

### Regional fairs and festivals
County fairs and small-town festivals frequently book country artists, creating a different experience from destination music festivals.

## How to choose a festival

Compare:

- camping versus hotel-based attendance;
- single-stage versus multi-stage format;
- mainstream country versus Texas/Red Dirt/Americana focus;
- family policies;
- heat exposure;
- parking/shuttle logistics;
- outside-chair/bag rules;
- weather and refund policy.

## Keep lineups out of evergreen body copy

Artist rosters change every year and are highly copyrighted/promotional content. The final page should use a current-year table or callout generated only after official lineup verification.
