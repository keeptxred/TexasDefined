# Historic Texas Theaters Still Hosting Live Shows

Texas has restored movie palaces, vaudeville houses and community theaters that remain active performance venues. This guide should focus on buildings with documented historic significance and current live programming.

## What qualifies

A venue should have a historic building or theater legacy, documented preservation/restoration history and an active schedule that includes live performances rather than only private rentals.

## Theater types to include

### Restored movie palaces
Many early-20th-century theaters survive because local nonprofits or cities restored them for concerts, film and touring shows.

### Opera houses and vaudeville-era venues
Smaller Texas towns sometimes retain late-19th- or early-20th-century performance buildings with ongoing programming.

### Community theaters in historic buildings
A building can remain culturally important even if the current producing organization is much newer than the structure.

## What readers need to know

- Current performance calendar
- Seating/accessibility details
- Parking and downtown access
- Building tours, if any
- Restoration history
- Whether the theater is nonprofit, municipal or privately operated

## Internal-link plan

Link to restored theaters, live-theater destinations, downtown/small-town guides, architecture and city pages.

## Verification notes

Verify current operations and live-show schedules directly with each theater. Use THC, National Register documentation and local preservation organizations for architectural history. Do not assume a historic theater is still open because its building survives.

## Image guidance

Use one rights-cleared marquee, auditorium or historic exterior image. Verify whether interior photography is permitted.
