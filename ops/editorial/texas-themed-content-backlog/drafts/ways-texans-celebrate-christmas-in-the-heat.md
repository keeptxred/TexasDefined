# Ways Texans Celebrate Christmas When It’s Warm Outside

Texas Christmas weather can range from freezing to short-sleeve afternoons. The joke works best when it celebrates flexibility instead of claiming the state is always hot in December.

## Warm-weather holiday scenes

- Outdoor light displays without snow boots
- Patio meals when a warm front cooperates
- Barbecue or smoked meats alongside traditional holiday dishes
- Tamales as a major seasonal tradition in many families
- Coastal towns using boats and waterfront lights
- Kids riding new bikes or scooters immediately outside
- Holiday markets held in open-air downtowns
- Santa photos with palm trees or live oaks nearby
- Backyard games after lunch
- Switching from heat to air conditioning and back again during one week
- Ice rinks and artificial snow becoming novelty attractions
- Hill Country light trails and town-square events

## Weather reality

A warm Christmas one year does not predict the next. Current event and safety copy must be refreshed from local organizers and NWS forecasts.
