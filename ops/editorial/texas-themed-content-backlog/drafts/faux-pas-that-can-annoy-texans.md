# Faux Pas That Can Annoy Texans

This is etiquette humor, not a list of things guaranteed to offend every Texan.

## Lighthearted faux pas

1. Assuming the entire state is desert.
2. Treating Dallas, Fort Worth, Austin, Houston and San Antonio as interchangeable.
3. Calling every smoked meat “Texas barbecue” without understanding regional styles.
4. Blocking the left lane while not passing.
5. Trampling or picking roadside bluebonnets for a photo.
6. Assuming everyone owns cattle, boots or a cowboy hat.
7. Telling someone a three-hour drive is “basically across the state.”
8. Pronouncing a well-known local town name with total confidence and no curiosity.
9. Confusing Tex-Mex with all Mexican food—or vice versa.
10. Ignoring heat, flood or severe-weather warnings because the sky looks fine where you are.
11. Treating small-town festivals or Friday-night football as silly when they matter deeply to the community.
12. Saying Texas has no natural scenery without having seen its different regions.

## Editorial note

Keep this away from politics, identity policing and demographic stereotypes. The humor should target assumptions and visitor behavior.
