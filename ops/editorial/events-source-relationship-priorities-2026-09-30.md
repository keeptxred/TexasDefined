# TexasDefined Events source and relationship priorities

Review date: 2026-09-30
Scope: Events only

Purpose: expand first-party event discovery and create legitimate source relationships in the regional corridors where TexasDefined remains thinnest. This is a research/relationship queue, not a bulk-email list. No outreach is sent by this document.

## Operating rules

- Use destination marketing organizations, city tourism offices and public calendars as discovery layers unless they are themselves the event owner.
- Resolve each candidate event to the organizer, host venue or public institution before using dates, cancellation status, ticket rules or operating details as canonical facts.
- Do not scrape or republish event photography without item-level reuse rights.
- Prefer relationships that can provide official photography, correction channels, recurring date announcements and source attribution.
- Ask for a backlink/reference only when TexasDefined already has a useful, accurate destination/event guide worth citing.
- Do not infer annual dates from prior years.

## Priority 1 — Panhandle / High Plains

### Visit Amarillo

Why it matters:

- Official destination calendar with current local events and an annual-events section.
- Strong fit for Panhandle event discovery, especially Amarillo-area festivals, rodeos, Route 66 programming, arts and museum events.
- The organization explicitly maintains partner/media infrastructure and invites collaboration/feature requests.

Useful public channels:

- `info@visitamarillo.com` — partner/account access channel.
- `jackie@visitamarillo.com` — public "Get Featured" contact shown on the partner page.
- `stephanie@visitamarillo.com` — public meetings/marketing contact shown by Visit Amarillo.

Best relationship ask when outreach is authorized:

1. establish TexasDefined as a source-aware regional event guide;
2. ask for a recurring press/event update channel;
3. request rights-cleared destination/event photography with explicit reuse terms;
4. offer correction/update access for Amarillo event guides;
5. only after a strong Amarillo guide exists, ask whether their media/visitor resources page accepts useful third-party planning references.

### Visit Lubbock

Why it matters:

- Official events calendar with a strong West Texas arts/music identity.
- High-value source for Lubbock Arts Festival, Lubbock Uncorked, 4th on Broadway, Buddy Holly programming, ranching/cowboy events and other recurring anchors.

Useful public channels:

- `hello@visitlubbock.org`
- `media@marketlubbock.org`

Best relationship ask:

- recurring media/event notices;
- official event-owner introductions where Visit Lubbock is only the discovery source;
- rights-cleared destination photography;
- correction channel for TexasDefined's Lubbock/Panhandle event coverage.

## Priority 2 — Big Bend / Far West

### Visit MARFA / City of Marfa tourism

Why it matters:

- Public current event calendar with named annual and destination-scale events including Marfa Lights Festival and Viva Big Bend.
- City tourism department is a practical regional relationship target because TexasDefined's Big Bend page already has early page-one GSC visibility but needs stronger permanent-guide depth.

Useful public channel:

- `contact@visitmarfa.com`

Important rights note:

- Visit MARFA's published terms state that site content may not be reproduced or distributed without prior written permission. Treat their photos/text as source material only unless written reuse permission is granted.

Best relationship ask:

- a standing event-update/correction relationship;
- permission to use selected tourism/event images with explicit written rights and credit requirements;
- introductions to event owners for larger annual events;
- a future reference/backlink request only after TexasDefined's Marfa/Big Bend guides are sufficiently useful to visitors.

### Visit Alpine / Alpine Visitor Center

Why it matters:

- Current annual-events material and an active events calendar.
- Strong source for Big Bend Bluegrass Festival and other Alpine/Far West recurring events.

Useful public channel:

- `visitorcenter@visitalpinetx.com`

Best relationship ask:

- current annual-event schedule notices;
- event-owner contacts;
- rights-cleared Alpine/Big Bend imagery;
- correction/update pathway for TexasDefined's Far West guides.

Relationship priority:

## Relationship scoring model

Score future outreach candidates using five dimensions, each 0-2:

1. **Authority:** first-party DMO/public institution or direct organizer.
2. **Regional gap value:** materially improves a thin TexasDefined region.
3. **Photography value:** plausible access to reusable official media.
4. **Recurring-update value:** organization can provide repeat annual/current notices.
5. **Citation/backlink plausibility:** TexasDefined has or can build a guide their visitors would reasonably use.

Prioritize 8-10 point targets first. Do not email every listed address; choose the single most relevant public contact per organization when outreach is authorized.

## Publication gate

A candidate from this queue does not become a permanent TexasDefined guide until it has:

- a first-party source;
- current verified occurrence data or a clearly evergreen entity state with no unsupported scheduled schema;
- source review date;
- enough planning value to justify a permanent page;
- image status that satisfies existing rights governance or correctly fails closed to `noindex, follow`.
