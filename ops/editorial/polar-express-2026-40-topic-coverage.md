## Page-splitting and publication gate

- A new URL may be created only when it adds independently confirmed information that would be cumbersome or misleading on the canonical page (e.g. a detailed verified hotel-and-depot itinerary with an original map and current opening hours).
- Do not generate one page for each audience segment, nearby city, day or ticket type by paraphrasing the same operator details.
- Every published page must have first-party sources, truly distinctive editorial reporting, verified internal links, appropriate photo provenance, schema confidence and the repository's canonical premerge check.
- Dates and ticket links link to the respective official railroad, not Ticketmaster unless the specific event is verified as a first-party Ticketmaster listing. Nearby hotels and tours may use existing audited affiliate modules with disclosure.
- Update 2027 season dates only when both organizers confirm the next calendar; archive 2026 details as history rather than cloning new 2027 pages.
