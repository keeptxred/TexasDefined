## Differences resolved from primary evidence

1. **Two age policies, not one.** General Moody Gardens admissions use its own children 4–12 and under-4 terms. The Galveston Children's Museum separately applies general admission to visitors **age 2+**. Do not imply children 2–3 are free at the museum merely because they can be free for some Moody Gardens attraction tickets.
2. **Two membership-hours rules, not one.** The museum's 9–10 a.m. hour is for **GCM members**; Moody Gardens members may enter during regular public hours, subject to the museum's published admission terms.
3. **Admission is specific to what is purchased.** An Aquarium or Rainforest ticket alone is not proof of museum entry; confirm membership, museum-specific admission or an inclusive pass at checkout.
4. **Holiday opening is site-specific.** Moody Gardens may be open while the children's museum is closed on a listed holiday. The museum also posts potential seasonal extended hours; use its own current schedule.
5. **Nov. 16 early closing is temporary.** Official operator advisory says attraction last entry at 1 p.m. before a 2 p.m. planned outage. The content uses a server-time-gated notice expiring **2026-11-17 06:00 UTC / midnight CST** and must be checked live on both public origins on the dated workflow.
6. **Do not imply a third-party Galveston ghost tour sells Moody Gardens admission.** The venue guide uses direct official admission links, rather than a citywide affiliate module on this specific attraction.

## Original editorial choices (no fabricated experience)

- One-pyramid/two-pyramid/full-day options are editorial planning **estimates**, not operator-promised durations or a report from a personal visit.
- The full $60 family example assumes 4 visitors all age 2+ using undiscounted museum general admission on the stated review date; it excludes Moody Gardens attraction tickets, taxes, special events and possible promotions.
- The Aquarium's marine conservation exhibits, Rainforest's warm/humid free-roaming habitat and Discovery's two different experiences give readers a meaningful basis to choose or decline an attraction.
- Three geographically grounded follow-ups and links to current official operator checkout, accessibility, food/parking, hours and museum details are more useful than statewide generic article feeds.
- Affiliate widgets for generic Galveston tours are suppressed for this exact attraction, which is not represented as a reseller of those city tours.
