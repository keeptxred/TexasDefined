# Authentic-image resolution decisions — 2026-10-14

Scope: two specific heritage authority destinations in the 2026-10-14 image-and-Discover production audit. The decisions below are **editorial acceptance of authentic, properly attributed source photos**, **not exemptions from production image checks** and not claims of Google Discover large-image eligibility.

## Stephen F. Austin Experimental Forest — authentic 800 × 600 USDA bridge image

Canonical: https://texasdefined.com/destination/stephen-f-austin-experimental-forest

- Exact current page hero: https://research.fs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2023-02/srs-sfaef-bridge.jpg
- Operator and photographic context: https://research.fs.usda.gov/srs/forestsandranges/locations/austin
- Subject/identity: the USDA Forest Service's own bridge on the Stephen F. Austin Experimental Forest interpretive trail; correctly labeled as a contemporary official-site photograph, **not** a substitute image from a different forest.
- Rights: official USDA Forest Service Southern Research Station photo, a US federal-government work (verify per-item rights again before any derivative publication).
- Native 800 × 600 is below the 1,200px preferred Discover image width. Searches on October 14 did not identify an independently source-confirmed higher-resolution original of **this forest** with reuse rights. The related university forestry program photos do not establish that the image was taken at this experimental forest.
- **Accepted decision:** keep the genuine 800 × 600 image; do not upscale, stretch, claim it is 1,200px, swap in a photograph of an unrelated forest, or remove the image quality signal from automated audits.
- **Open Discover requirement:** obtain an actual larger, appropriately licensed image of this very forest from the operator or another documented rights holder, or separately determine the appropriate indexing/image-publication treatment. A photo does not become large-image compliant by editorial acceptance.
