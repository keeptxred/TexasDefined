# TexasDefined — site-wide publication and optional editorial certification policy

**Owner directive:** October 20, 2026 (America/Chicago). **Scope:** ALL TexasDefined pages, including football, softball, county, city, destinations, attractions, parks, museums, news, guides, property data, fishing, events, shopping, and future URL families. This policy supersedes any older page-category requirement that a named human editor/owner complete a nine-dimensional acceptance form or a separate formal editorial approval before an otherwise ready page can be published, technical-VERIFIED, or advanced past its batch. It is not a Batch 006-only exception.

## Release is not a formal editorial certificate

**Required before saying a page is published/technically complete:** individually researched original content suitable for the subject; claims tied to appropriate dated and attributable primary sources or clearly marked unknown; correct entity/season/campus; truthful and legally usable assets (or an explicitly labeled original nonphotographic alternative); reasonable source-date/freshness assessment; valid metadata, canonical, structured data and relevant internal links; passing applicable build/CI, responsive, rendering and accessibility automation; protected merge where required; and actual live production evidence. The evidence scope and any missing fact must be recorded, not fabricated. Only perform tests appropriate to the changed pages; do not restart disabled site-wide crawls.

**Not required as a blocking dependency:** separate named owner approval, a formal human editorial sign-off, a nine-field Supabase `accept_authority_page` submission, `ownerApproved: true` manifest flag, a human/assistive-technology audit beyond the demonstrated automated checks, or documentary photography where genuine licensed photos cannot be obtained and truthful original graphics/illustrations are appropriate. A missing optional reviewer or unlicensed/unavailable school photograph does not itself keep correctly published, source-supported, technically tested pages in an unfinished batch.

**Never relax the underlying safety and quality checks:** no invented facts, game scores, access/ADA assurances, fabricated reviews or citations; no unlicensed photographs/logos/trademarks or private student imagery; no fake human approvals; no claiming a keyboard/axe test is a full human WCAG certification; no passing failed CI, absent live proof or incorrect dates. Where a claim cannot be established, qualify it or omit it. Rights for any asset actually used remain compulsory.

## Separate status dimensions, for EVERY page family

- **Source-backed publication:** what the code/metadata and live production show. Distinguish `DRAFT`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `MERGED`, `DEPLOYED`, and `TECHNICALLY_VERIFIED` using actual evidence. A batch can finish its delivery when every assigned page meets its scoped live technical acceptance, without waiting for optional formal editorial paperwork.
- **Optional editorial certification:** `td_authority_page_acceptance.status='accepted'` and `td_maintenance.accept_authority_page` continue to mean that a **real** named reviewer completed their specified evidence review. An `in_progress` or `needs_research` row means no optional editorial certification is recorded, **not** that a technically verified published URL must be withheld or an entire batch is blocked.
- **Factual/source monitoring:** `td_authority_verification_registry`, registered sources and monitoring intervals remain active. Source probes are source-reachability signals, not fact checks; `last_verified_at` and reviewer identity must never be fabricated. Missing/changed sources should trigger prioritised correction and appropriate public qualification, not automatic fake sign-off.
- **Rights/accessibility:** record real media permissions and actual test coverage. `mediaRightsReviewed=false` or an unperformed manual assistive-technology review is a disclosed outstanding enhancement, never an implicit license or an invalidation of source-backed original graphics.

This owner-directed rule applies to central authority standards and category-specific release manifests alike. New validators must test documentary/source/media/technical correctness and may accept records with no `ownerApproved` flag or human-review form. Preserve any tighter legal, privacy, commerce or security approvals unrelated to editorial certification; this rule does not authorize bypassing them.

**Historical records are not rewritten.** Previous unsuccessful manual-review attempts remain truthful in their dated reports, while the operational blocker is superseded. Existing optional Supabase review functions and audit dashboards can remain available; their legacy `accepted_current` semantics describe human certification, **not** the authoritative publication or deployment status. Never bulk-mark thousands of records `accepted`, update review timestamps without review, or present source uptime as editorial sign-off.
