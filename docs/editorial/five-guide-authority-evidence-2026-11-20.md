## Material discrepancies resolved

1. **Hueco Tanks:** TPWD [main park page](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/hueco-tanks) describes 70 daily self-guided permits and a minimum one-week guided-tour booking. Its [Activities page](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/hueco-tanks/activities) describes 70 people *at one time*, and advises early advance requests for a confirmed guide. Do **not** collapse these into one claim. New copy explicitly discloses the different scopes; uses the one-week minimum for guided tours, the mandatory orientation and the phone-only self-guided permit at (512) 389-8911. No automatic permit availability is implied.
2. **Emily D. West:** The [Texas State Library record](https://www.tsl.texas.gov/exhibits/texas175/emilywest.html) supports a passport request and her historical existence; it does **not** independently date the request to a specific year in this presentation nor corroborate the later Yellow Rose/San Jacinto story. Remove the unsupported date from the original evidence panel.
3. **Ticket prices:** [Twin Sisters calendar](https://twinsistersdancehall.com/calendar/) shows New Year's Eve December 31, 2026 adult $15 / child (11 or younger) $5, with separate table/presale options; label as a named performance, not a permanent rate. [Gruene Hall FAQ](https://gruenehall.com/faq/) explicitly says free across-the-street parking, no guaranteed chair/dance space and show-format variation.
4. **Mum products:** [Melz original DIY kit catalog](https://www.melzmumz.com/online-store/Melz-Mum-DIY-Kits-c52590977) shows $79 single / $86 double. [Classic made-to-order model](https://www.melzmumz.com/online-store/Classic-Single-Double-Triple-%26-Quad-Homecoming-Mums-p204726247) shows $139 before added options. The [Deluxe Single Garter](https://www.melzmumz.com/deluxe-single-garter) lists $139. [Mum Factory Outlet](https://www.mumfactoryoutlet.com/) directly displays a 10-inch mum flower for $8.50, three blank round backers for $2.99 and a sheet of rhinestone letters for $10.00; that $21.49 subtotal is not a finished mum and excludes ribbon, attachments, other decor, tax and delivery. Prices are dated examples, not accepted custom quotations.
5. **Climbing safety:** [Lake Mineral Wells](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/lake-mineral-wells/rock-climbing) requires registration and waiver each visit, bans bouldering/free/solo climbing, applies minor parent/guardian rules, and restricts groups to approved guides. [Enchanted Rock](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/enchanted-rock/more-info/rock-climb) warns that some rappels need 70-meter ropes and protects traditional routes. Don't turn the general length into a universal sufficient rope specification.
6. **Accessibility:** Photos and SVGs provide alt/role labels. A guide's parking lot accessibility is not the same as accessibility of its canyon, rock route or trail. We make only operator-grounded accessible-route claims and ask the venue when unknown; synthetic Playwright keyboard checks are not a WCAG manual certification.
