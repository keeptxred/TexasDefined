# Post Oak Bend historical nonlevy evidence

**Classification:** Year-specific verified local supplements, not modifications
to the Comptroller's original statewide reporting rows.

- **2021:** Kaufman CAD's [2021 Certified Totals](https://kaufman-cad.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/2021-Certified-Totals.pdf),
  grand totals page 211/588 (PDF page index 210), identifies the
  CPO City of Post Oak Bend with a 0.000000 city tax rate and $0
  approximate levy on $81,019,286 taxable valuation.
- **2024:** The city's [May 14, 2024 council minutes](https://www.postoakbend.org/Minutes%205-14-2024.pdf)
  describe the introduction of a city property tax as a potential
  future road-maintenance funding option. The
  [current mayor's description](https://postoakbend.org/mayor/)
  corroborates that the city was still funded without a city property tax
  in 2025. This is evidence of **no municipal levy in 2024**, not
  evidence that any individual property owed zero taxes to other units.
- **2025:** Previously verified via the city's direct statement.
  Its 2025 supplement remains as already published.

The 2021 and 2024 Comptroller records remain `not-reported`
with null original numeric rates. Separately sourced city zero
rates may display on the taxing-unit history page; school, county,
ESD and other property taxes are not represented by those zeros.

Do not infer a 2022 or 2023 city levy without suitable year-specific
primary evidence and the corresponding verified source record.
