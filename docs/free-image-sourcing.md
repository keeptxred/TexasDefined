# TexasDefined Free Image Sourcing

Use this source list whenever TexasDefined needs a free-to-use photo, historical image, illustration, or map. Prefer an exact match for the subject over generic Texas imagery, and verify the rights statement on the individual asset before publishing.

## Search sources

1. **Wikimedia Commons** — photos, historical images, architecture, maps, and public-domain material  
   Search: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search={QUERY}&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image`  
   Rights rule: only use files whose individual file page permits reuse (for example public domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA). Preserve creator and license attribution when required.

2. **The Portal to Texas History** — Texas historical photographs, maps, documents, architectural material, and primary sources  
   Search: `https://texashistory.unt.edu/search/?q={QUERY}&t=fulltext`  
   Rights rule: check the item's **Citations, Rights, Re-Use** information before using it. Do not assume every Portal item is public domain.

3. **PICRYL** — public-domain historical media aggregated from libraries, archives, and government collections  
   Topic search: `https://picryl.com/topics/{QUERY}`  
   Rights rule: use only items whose asset page explicitly states public domain, CC0, or another reuse status suitable for TexasDefined. Confirm the original source when available.

4. **Unsplash** — modern travel and destination photography  
   Search: `https://unsplash.com/s/photos/{QUERY}`  
   Rights rule: use only images specifically available under the Unsplash License. Exclude Unsplash+, Getty, sponsored, or other premium/licensed results.

5. **Pixabay** — modern travel, landscape, architecture, and general-purpose stock photography  
   Search: `https://pixabay.com/images/search/{QUERY}/`  
   Rights rule: use assets covered by the Pixabay Content License. Exclude sponsored third-party/iStock results.

6. **OpenStreetMap** — maps only, not a general photography source  
   Search: `https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query={QUERY}`  
   Rights rule: display required OpenStreetMap attribution and follow OpenStreetMap's data/license and tile-usage requirements. Do not bulk scrape or prefetch public OSM tiles.

## Selection rules

- Prefer a photograph of the exact attraction, lake, park, venue, county landmark, town, or subject.
- For historical sections, prioritize true historical/public-domain material when it materially improves the article.
- For modern travel pages, prefer recent, accurate photography over vintage imagery unless the vintage image is intentionally illustrating history.
- Never publish an image merely because it appeared in Google Images. Follow the result to the original source and verify reuse rights there.
- Record the original source URL, creator/photographer when available, license or rights statement, and required attribution at selection time.
- Do not use a source when the rights statement is missing, contradictory, or too ambiguous for commercial publication.
- Existing automated Wikimedia Commons searches must continue to enforce license filtering; this broader list supplements Commons with additional approved search destinations.
