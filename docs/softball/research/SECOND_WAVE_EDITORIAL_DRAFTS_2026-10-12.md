## Jonesboro Lady Eagles — three consecutive 1A titles, a new district lookup

There are two entirely different Jonesboro softball searches a careless researcher could make. The correct school here is **Jonesboro Independent School District in Jonesboro, Texas**, whose official site is [jonesboroisd.net](https://www.jonesboroisd.net/), not Jonesboro, Arkansas or that city's recreational girls league. Jonesboro ISD's [May 30, 2026 school announcement](https://www.jonesboroisd.net/live_feeds/12474129) independently confirms the Lady Eagles' **third consecutive UIL 1A softball state championship**, spanning 2024, 2025 and 2026. That is a school-issued historical sports achievement, not a generic guess from a similar mascot.

Its [2025–26 UIL state-team profile](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/jonesboro-2025-2026-softball) lists coach **James Turner**, assistants **Keirsten Seahorn** and **Matt Dossey**, a submitted season record of **23–9–1**, and three straight appearances. Jonesboro ISD [also announced June 2026 THSCA Super Elite honors](https://www.jonesboroisd.net/live_feeds/12590620); these awards are separate from the UIL championship's all-tournament team. Individual students should be referred to through the official published awards, without copying roster contact data.

The future-looking district field is easily mishandled: **the official 2026–27 UIL softball 2A alignment** places Jonesboro in **District 26, Region IV**, alongside Bartlett, Bremond, Bruceville-Eddy, Chilton, Granger, Holland, Moody and Rosebud-Lott. It is still correct to describe Jonesboro's *2026 championship as 1A* because the title came from that historical bracket; it is not correct to relabel the 2024–26 trophies as current 2A titles.

## Grandview Zebras — consecutive 3A title seasons and a verifiable coach continuity

Grandview's [2025–26 UIL softball team sheet](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/grandview-2025-2026-softball) supplies a dated coaching record: **Leigh Ebner** as head coach with **Tim Ward** and **Allie Bennett** as assistants. The separate [2024–25 UIL sheet](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/grandview-2024-2025-softball) shows that same group during the prior state championship season. Those records make coaching continuity over the two *historic* seasons a legitimate fact. They cannot establish future Spring 2027 contracts or imply who originally founded the Grandview softball program.

The original 2026 UIL archive lists a submitted **35–3–1** tournament record and its fifth state-tournament appearance, with previous visits in **2018, 2023, 2024 and 2025**. Grandview's 2025 3A Division I title and 2026 3A Division I title should be described with each year's UIL state bracket labels. A state-tournament appearance does not mean the team won that season's title; counting them accurately is part of a useful historical timeline.

Grandview ISD provides a direct [school-controlled softball resource](https://gvisd.org/softballschedule). That is stronger as a current-source pathway than a user-editable score aggregator, but the presence of a team photograph on the school page **does not license TexasDefined to redistribute it**. Grandview ISD's administrative address on South Fifth Street cannot be assumed to locate the school varsity softball field entrance or accessible seats. Event planning remains a separate verification project.

The 2026–27 softball [3A alignment](https://www.uiltexas.org/files/alignments/263ASB.pdf) places Grandview in **District 16, Region II**, with Blooming Grove, Keene, Maypearl, Mildred, Rice and Rio Vista. These are district peers based on the sport-specific current alignment, not Grandview football opponents.

**Outstanding before publication:** authenticated school venue, school-issued spring fixture schedule, original chronology before 2018, college alumni and community reporting, image permission, 38-topic audit and full production browser acceptance.

## Santa Gertrudis Academy Lady Lions — school and university must not be conflated

The source-identification trap for Santa Gertrudis Academy is unusual. The [school's official high-school profile](https://www.sgisd.net/apps/pages/index.jsp?pREC_ID=1262603&type=d&uREC_ID=928070) locates Santa Gertrudis Academy High School at **1055 West Santa Gertrudis Street, Kingsville**, in Poteet Hall on the **Texas A&M University–Kingsville campus**. This is a Texas public high school within Santa Gertrudis ISD, **not a college softball team**. Sharing an academic setting does not establish use of university softball facilities, university athletic results, or an NCAA conference affiliation.

The [UIL 2025–26 Lady Lions team record](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/santa-gertrudis-academy-2025-2026-softball) names **Thomas De Los Santos** as softball coach, with **Daniel Fernandez, Kennedy Silva and Amanda Sanchez** assisting. The school has an independent [current athletics staff directory](https://www.sgisd.net/apps/pages/index.jsp?pREC_ID=1714604&type=d&uREC_ID=930817) listing De Los Santos as **girls athletic coordinator, head volleyball and head softball coach**. This is stronger evidence of his current school role than carrying an archived 2026 line forward without verifying, but even the current directory cannot establish a published competition-by-competition spring-2027 schedule.

The UIL sheet records state appearances in **2013, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2023 and 2026** and the 2018 and 2026 title eras. For 2026, the historical bracket labels the title **3A Division II**; the new UIL alignment lists its school name as **Santa Gertrudis Acad** in **3A District 30, Region IV**. A complete authority page must explicitly resolve the abbreviation to the school's full campus identity rather than mistake it for another organization.

**Outstanding before publication:** separate actual home softball diamond and gate, campus visitor restrictions, school-specific past championships and coaching dates, local archival reporting, licensed team/venue images, the full 13 distinct research sections and source review.

## Bullard Panthers — a first 2026 title and a school-owned broadcast resource

The 2025–26 UIL tournament sheet places [Bullard](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/bullard-2025-2026-softball) in the **2026 4A Division I champion** position under **Julie Murry**, with assistant coaches **Brett Murry, Danielle Hudspeth and Callie Bailey**. The team's prior state-tournament appearances appear in **2021 and 2022**, while 2026 is its first title among those entries. Do not extend that softball milestone into unsupported claims about championships for Bullard's other sports or label its 36–7–1 UIL-submitted record a verified updated postseason final.

[Bullard ISD's own Panther Sports Net information](https://www.bullardisd.net/community/panther-sports-net) mentions home softball-field streaming via a third-party Hudl service. Its page separately lists Panther Stadium, a gym, baseball and softball playing areas. This distinction is important: the fact that a varsity softball contest can be watched through the school's authorized school broadcast partner does **not** prove it is played inside the football stadium or provide an independently checked parking map. Nor does a video link confer photography or broadcast redistribution rights on TexasDefined. Subscriptions/prices listed on the current source should be treated as seasonal terms that may change.

UIL's [2026–27 4A softball alignment](https://www.uiltexas.org/files/alignments/264ASB.pdf) records Bullard in **District 17, Region III** with Center, Hudson, Jacksonville, Madisonville, Palestine and Rusk. That gives the future district-opponents section genuine school-specific data before any extrapolation from other sports. The archive needs additional original research before it can explain the program's early history, 2021 and 2022 seasons, coaching careers, youth development and community role.

**Outstanding before publication:** actual diamond location and accessibility, verified 2027 coaching/schedules, independently checked first title celebration, school history and local media, licensed field photograph, editorial QA and approval.

## Liberty Lady Panthers — distinguish the program from Texarkana's Liberty-Eylau

Liberty's school-identity risk is acute because **Liberty ISD in Liberty, Texas** and **Liberty-Eylau ISD in Texarkana** are separate districts, not aliases. The correct public-school operator uses [libertyisd.net](https://www.libertyisd.net/) and its UIL team identity is the **Lady Panthers**. Any search result mentioning Liberty-Eylau sports schedules, a Leopards mascot or a Texarkana field must be rejected rather than incorporated because both names share the word Liberty.

The authentic [Liberty 2025–26 UIL softball team sheet](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/liberty-2025-2026-softball) names **Karen Slack** as historical head coach and **Joe Slack, Katherine McAdams and Maci Beam** as assistants. Its chronological tournament-appearance list includes **2018, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026**. Earlier title markers on 2018, 2021 and 2022, followed by the 2026 4A Division II championship, give four identified championship seasons to research from original UIL final brackets. The archived **38–6** record is a dated state-entry figure, not necessarily the post-final season total.

The correct **2026–27 softball alignment** is **4A District 19, Region III**, shared with Hamshire-Fannett, Hardin-Jefferson, Huffman Hargrave, Livingston and Shepherd. These district opponents are not evidence about current football alignment or the eventual 2027 softball final standings.

A genuinely complete Liberty page needs an authoritative description of its playing diamond and public-access policy, community heritage and proven softball title history. Merely repeating its state championship years across 13 sections, or copying athletics contact details from the Texarkana district, would be exactly the sort of false authority and boilerplate we are avoiding.
