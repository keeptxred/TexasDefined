## Barbers Hill Eagles — championships and an unusually precise official softball venue description

Mont Belvieu Barbers Hill's history cannot be represented accurately by showing the present alignment next to its three titles. In the [UIL state champion archive](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/archives), the Eagles' **2021 championship is recorded in 5A**, while the **2025 and 2026 titles are 5A Division I**. The team's [2025–26 state tournament sheet](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/mont-belvieu-barbers-hill-2025-2026-softball) independently documents six tournament appearances — 2006, 2014, 2018, 2021, 2025 and 2026 — and identifies head coach Aaron Fuller, with Jared Smith, Sarah Aguilar and Lacey Eads as assistants for that historical team. Those six *appearances* are not six championships, and the 39–5 record on that sheet was submitted for the state tournament rather than certified as the final post-title total. The [Barbers Hill school scoreboard](https://athletics.bhisd.net/inside-athletics/scoreboard) records the 2026 title game: an **11–0 win over Aledo**. The [UIL 2026 all-tournament awards](https://www.uiltexas.org/press-releases/detail/2026-uil-softball-state-all-tournament-teams) identify Macie Bryant as the TGCA-selected all-tournament MVP for 5A Division I. None of these retrospective records should be repurposed as a 2027 roster.

The identity change that readers need explained is in the **official 2026–27 softball alignment**, where *Mt Belvieu Barbers Hill* is a **6A District 18, Region III** program, notwithstanding the preceding 5A Division I championship. Its historical titles remain historically classified. The [current school athletic staff directory](https://athletics.bhisd.net/inside-athletics/athletics-staff) lists Aaron Fuller as head softball coach, and [its schedules directory](https://athletics.bhisd.net/inside-athletics/schedules) contains a 2026–27 softball entry. Those pages are useful primary resources but still need event-by-event confirmation before the first team page is signed off.

Barbers Hill offers stronger real-world visitor evidence than most schools in the early pilots. The district's [official athletic-facilities guide](https://athletics.bhisd.net/inside-athletics/athletic-facilities) expressly identifies a **Softball Field** *behind the high school, next to the baseball field*, about three-quarters of a mile along FM 3180 from Interstate 10 Exit 800; it reports **440 seats**. The page also lists the district athletic complex at **9696 Eagle Drive**, although its main venue address labels the city *Baytown* and its footer calls it *Mont Belvieu*. Do not silently resolve that discrepancy into a precise field gate coordinate. Most rules on that page concern **Eagle Stadium**, a football venue; its wheelchair-viewing arrangement, bag rules and stadium entrances cannot be copied onto the softball field without softball-specific confirmation. This is exactly the type of mistaken venue join that spoiled earlier football pages.

**Still required before a public page:** exact diamond ingress and accessible route, verified parking/admissions, authoritative 2027 staff/game schedule and enrollment, campus county/TEA identity, pre-2006 original program history, fully documented title/playoff/coach chronology, authentic reusable field photography with license, and original school/community/alumni coverage. The team remains research-only.

## Lake Creek Lions — a documented program birth, four championships and a misleading legacy staff page

The [Montgomery ISD June 23, 2026 school-board recap](https://www.misd.org/news/what_s_new/june_23__2026_board_meeting_recap) offers unusually clear first-party chronology: Lake Creek's varsity softball program began play in **2019**, then secured Texas titles in **2022, 2023, 2025 and 2026**, with an attributed cumulative **268–23** program mark since 2019. That is a materially different date from Lake Creek High School's [August 21, 2018 campus opening](https://lakecreekhs.misd.org/). No responsible history should declare that the varsity softball team began in 2018 simply because the building opened then. The school board also supplies the **final 2026 season result of 40–1**, whereas [UIL's 2025–26 tournament-submission sheet](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/montgomery-lake-creek-2025-2026-softball) lists **39–1**, an earlier snapshot. Date labeling explains the difference without dismissing either official record.

Lake Creek's 2026 state trophy came in **5A Division II**, with the program in the **2026–27 5A District 17, Region III** softball alignment. [UIL's all-tournament release](https://www.uiltexas.org/press-releases/detail/2026-uil-softball-state-all-tournament-teams) names Madalyn Davis the 2026 championship all-tournament MVP. The historical [UIL team sheet](https://www.uiltexas.org/softball/state-team/montgomery-lake-creek-2025-2026-softball) names Candyce Carter as head coach with Kacy Spivey and Dylan Larsen as assistants. This remains a dated historical assertion. The [current Lake Creek official athletics directory](https://lakecreekhs.misd.org/for_students/athletics) separately lists Candyce Carter as its softball coach; that more targeted source helps distinguish a [legacy official faculty biography](https://lakecreekhs.misd.org/directory/faculty/michelle_rochinski) that continues to describe Michelle Rochinski as the head softball coach. The older biography should be kept as potentially historical material, not propagated as current staff. The date and nature of that transition still require independent evidence.

Facilities must be handled even more carefully. [Montgomery ISD's venue directory](https://www.misd.org/departments/athletics/other_athletic_venues) explicitly says its complex at 22628 TX-105 includes **Montgomery High School baseball and softball**; a separate Lake Creek campus listing alone does **not** identify the Lions' softball diamond or entrance. The school's main published campus address of **1711 Homeplace Boulevard** must not be substituted as an exact field entrance without support. This protects visitors from being sent to the wrong school or a football venue. A current varsity schedule and event policies also need direct confirmation.

**Still required before a public page:** lake-creek-specific softball field and entrance documentation, staff chronology/season confirmation, 2027 varsity/JV event and roster links, locally meaningful accessibility and admissions guidance, school identifiers/enrollment/county, deeper pre-championship and coaching archives, legitimate media rights and all 13 original substantial first-release sections. The program remains research-only.
