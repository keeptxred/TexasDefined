## Additional directly sourced historical context

The [Southland Conference's August 27, 2025 softball release](https://www.southland.org/news/2025/8/22/southland-conference-announces-2026-softball-schedule.aspx) says East Texas A&M had gained full Division I status, and provides original 2026 Southland participation. [Southland 2026 softball statistics](https://www.southland.org/stats.aspx?conf=true&path=softball&year=2026) and [Stephen F. Austin's original 2026 conference schedule](https://www.southland.org/schedule.aspx?schedule=2160) provide historical participation evidence for several Texas schools. Those **2026** standings do not alone prove that an institution retained the identical **2027** affiliation or that staff/venues are unchanged.

For every institution, collect the actual college athletics program URL, conference athletics page, 2027 season schedule or formal affiliation announcement, exact varsity sport sponsorship, current campus identity, team name, coach/staff snapshot, full official archive, facility/visitor facts, NCAA division, eligibility rules, sources, and licensed photographs. Keep conference transition history in dated fields, never overwritten with a new conference label for a historical trophy.

## Unfinished scope explicitly retained

The 38-row branch is **not an exhaustive 2026–27 Texas collegiate softball census**. It does not cover the whole NCAA III landscape and has not established a complete NAIA, NJCAA DI/DII/DIII or independent institutional listing. It must be checked against contemporary NCAA membership directories, each conference's official softball team listings and school athletics pages. Investigate all potentially omitted schools (including UTRGV as a **verification question**, never an asserted varsity softball program) and identify discontinued or newly sponsored sports from original department records. Separate NCAA and NJCAA recruiting pathways and roster/privacy practices.

Do not merge the old feature branch directly into current main until its changes are compared against today's optional softball route and safeguards: the branch predates most of the full-authority page engine. Retain only verified, non-destructive material; do not restore older route-tree files or publish generic college cards as original authority profiles. The hub's existing **selected** college directory is not evidence of a full census.

**Acceptance:** no public college program pages from these rows until individually validated facts, unique program histories, rights, facilities and complete reader utility are ready and production CI/SSR/browser QA pass.
