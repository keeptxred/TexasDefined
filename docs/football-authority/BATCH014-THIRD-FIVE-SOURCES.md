## Non-negotiable source distinctions

1. **South Oak Cliff** is Dallas ISD Golden Bears at Marsalis Avenue. Its name resembles Life School Oak Cliff, a separate charter whose Lions team and 4400 S. R. L. Thornton campus are wholly separate. The first-party school page identifies Golden Bear, not generic Bears.
2. **Spruce** currently uses Timberwolves, formerly Apaches per first-party quick facts. Spruce history dates the building to 1963 and distinguishes the original Rylie High. Its separately maintained coaching page `/student-services/athletics/coaching-staff` says Carl Richardson Jr is in his ninth head-coaching year and was selected athletic coordinator in 2017; this appears undated/historically written. **Do not extrapolate a current 2026–27 appointment** until confirmed on a dated current staffing page. Do not transfer an old team name into the current identity.
3. **Sunset** current school general information confirms Bison and purple/white school colors. School office address and operational hours are *not* stadium location, ticket or gameday access guidance.
4. **W. T. White** first-party school and Dallas ISD feeder data identify Longhorns and Ridgeside Drive, distinct from unrelated White schools. The 2026-11-05 school enrollment snapshot is enrollment context, not varsity roster.
5. **Woodrow Wilson** school identifies the Wildcats and its campus at Glasgow Drive. A school's athletics banner is not an official 2026 record or a license to reuse team logos/photos.
