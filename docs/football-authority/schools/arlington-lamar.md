## Unique missing school history and sources
The default football team data failed to present the Vikings' documented **1990 Class 5A Division II state-final appearance** correctly, current official coach, school colors and local venue guidance.

- [UIL official 1990 playoff archive](https://www.uiltexas.org/historical-archives/athletics/archives/football/playoff_text/90at_bfb.html) documents Lamar **49–7 El Paso Andress** in the state quarterfinal, **28–7 Dallas Carter** in the semifinal, then **10–27 Houston Aldine** in the final. [UIL state archive](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/archives/P376) separately corroborates **Houston Aldine 27–10 Arlington Lamar**. This is **one state final and zero state football championships**.
- [DCTF Lamar Vikings](https://www.texasfootball.com/team/arlington-lamar-vikings) attributes **29 football playoff appearances**, **2018 11–2**, **2019 10–2**, **2023 4–6**, **2024 4–6**, **2025 5–6**. Historical venue **Cravens Field** and 8,500 capacity are directory data, not verified current accessible gates or tickets.
- [Official Lamar High history](https://www.aisd.net/lamar-high-school/about/) establishes **Vikings**, **navy blue and gold**, school namesake and campus **1400 W. Lamar Boulevard**; school campus contact is not a verified stadium gate.
- [Official campus athletics staff](https://www.aisd.net/lamar-high-school/athletics/) identifies **Zach Najarro**, head football coach/athletics coordinator. This first-party record overrides stale older staffing lists.
- [2026 DCTF ledger](https://www.texasfootball.com/team/arlington-lamar-vikings) lists first five September results: Little Elm L10–30, Crowley W35–31, Justin Northwest W33–15, Bowie L23–29, Arlington High W41–31 = **3–2**. Weatherford Oct. 9 unverified in that snapshot.
