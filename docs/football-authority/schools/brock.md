## Source-level weaknesses fixed

- Brock's young-program chronology is unusual: started at junior high in **2011**, 2012 JV, 2013 varsity but not playoff-eligible (8–2), and first UIL district eligibility in 2014 (12–2). This requires its own origin narrative, not a generic mascot page.
- First UIL state championship in **2015** after an undefeated **16–0** campaign with Cameron Yoe final **43–33**. The UIL team record of 15–0 is **pre-championship** and does not contradict the district's final 16–0.
- Official UIL four championship finals: **2015 champion**, **2017 runner-up** to Rockdale 45–29, **2021 runner-up** to Lorena 35–18, **2022 runner-up** to Franklin 17–14. Abbreviated district summaries miss individual later-final years; no extra title is invented.
- Do **not** conflate historical 2015 title coach Chad Worrell with **2026 head coach Billy Mathis**. First-party staff lists **Chad Massey AD**.
- 2026 Brock 6–1 through Oct 16 from Oct 9 56–17 Glen Rose and Oct 17 77–0 Bridgeport post-game. MaxPreps's older aggregated fixture only **4–1** as of Oct 8. Brock ISD has scheduled games but no filled official result cells, so current-season totals are explicitly outside-reported.
- September 25 official school calendar lists **Monterrey Tech** as opponent, not MaxPreps's placeholder **Varsity Opponent**, for **63–7**; both descriptions shown in source notes.
- The high-school address 400 Eagle Spirit Lane is **not documented as the public stadium gate**; official varsity calendar supplies home/away, but parking, accessibility and ticket entrance remain unverified. Scrimmages Aug 13/20 are not 2026 W/L.
