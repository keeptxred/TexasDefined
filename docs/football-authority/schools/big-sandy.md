## Independently researched school story
**Big Sandy's 1973–75 dynasty, 1974 co-championship tie and Lovie Smith connection.** Official UIL records establish three consecutive Class B titles in 1973, 1974 and 1975 under coach Jim Norman, with 1974 decided 0–0 against Celina as a co-championship under historical rules. 1973 was 25–0 versus Rule. The UIL's champions list and 1975 archive say 26–2 over Groom but its centennial 1975 team account displays 28–2; the page explicitly flags the discrepancy instead of confidently selecting one from the same governing organization. UIL's centennial team biographies include Lovie Smith and David Overstreet among program football alumni for the 1973/1975 era. Big Sandy ISD published distinct varsity and junior-high 2026 football schedules in August.

- **1973 — First of three consecutive UIL Class B crowns**: Jim Norman coached the Wildcats to an unbeaten season, defeating Rule 25–0. UIL highlights David Overstreet and Lovie Smith on the title team's personnel list. Source: https://www.uiltexas.org/100/football-teams
- **1974 — Scoreless championship and official co-title with Celina**: The state game finished 0–0 and UIL names both Big Sandy and Celina co-champions. It was not an outright victory. Source: https://www.uiltexas.org/100/football
- **1975 — Third Class B state title with a disputed reported score**: Big Sandy beat Groom and completed its third consecutive title. UIL's historical winner list gives 26–2; UIL's own centennial roster account gives 28–2. This discrepancy is recorded for correction rather than silently picking one. Source: https://www.uiltexas.org/historical-archives/athletics/archives/football/champions.html
