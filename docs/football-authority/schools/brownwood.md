## Unique pre-audit deficiencies and findings

- Brownwood is not a generic mid-tier 4A page. Official UIL records credit **seven state football titles across eight title-game appearances**: championships **1960, 1965, 1967, 1969, 1970, 1978, 1981**, **1977 runner-up Dickinson 40–28**. This is an actual loss, not an eighth title. The old directory-level page did not correctly explain the distinction.
- UIL centennial records explicitly document **1965 Gordon Wood-coached, 14–0 Brownwood team**, 14–0 title win vs Bridge City, not a 1966 game. Its championship era is separate from current 2026 4A Division II.
- **Current coach Jeryl Brixey**, entering his second year in 2026, replaced former coach **Sammy Burnett** from older staff handbooks and search results. Local 2026 Brownwood reporting corroborates Brixey and 2025 team **7–5** with a fourth straight bi-district playoff victory.
- 2026 **2–5 through October 16** after 49–14 loss to La Vega and 20–14 loss to Robinson. An older MaxPreps schedule page remained **2–3** with no final results for Oct 9/16, yet later local KOXE and MaxPreps matchup reports confirm those played games. The **2–5** figure is dated source-attributed reporting and does not imply district-certified final season totals.
- High school campus **2100 Slayden, Brownwood, TX 76801** (school-owned page); Gordon Wood Stadium **3206 Milam Dr, Brownwood TX 76801** (district-owned stadium page). Its **7,500** capacity, artificial surface and general wheelchair access grade "Good" are from the district, but *specific accessible parking, companion seating, gate and lifts still require event confirmation*.
- The district **moved from previous 2025 ticketing to Hudl Ticketing for 2026**, and school-news release gives a 2026 summer season package of $60 seat-back / $50 bench and parking passes. These are not the correct price for a particular October single-game ticket. Use first-party `/tickets` and event-specific athletics page; 2025 ticket and student ID rules may not all be unchanged for 2026.
- Brownwood homecoming **October 23 Gatesville** at **7:00 p.m.** with Gordon Wood Hall of Champions recognitions; do not mark future game as a final.
