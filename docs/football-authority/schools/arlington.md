## School-specific missing coverage and primary research

The shared UIL directory gave Arlington High the same thin football treatment as many larger schools, omitting a documented **1951 UIL state championship**, school roots, original school identity, official current coach, and distinctions from five separate Arlington-area programs in this batch.

- [Arlington High official school history](https://www.aisd.net/arlington-high-school/about/history/) says the school selected **Colts** mascot and **kelly green and white** colors in **1923**. It identifies a **1951 Class AA football state championship over Waco La Vega**. Its motto/identity 'First Here; Best Here' has specific alumni relevance.
- [UIL all-time football appearances](https://wwwprod.uiltexas.org/football/all-time-appearances) confirms **Arlington one state football title and one appearance (1951)**. [Texas Prep Football](https://usaprepfootball.com/team/arlington-colts/) attributes the 1951 title-game score **7–0**, a third-party figure to confirm directly with archival UIL bracket before presenting as fully primary-verified.
- [Official Arlington High staff biography](https://www.aisd.net/arlington-high-school/campus-staff/scott-peach/) identifies **Scott Peach** as head football coach and athletic coordinator. [Arlington High official athletics directory](https://www.aisd.net/arlington-high-school/staff-department/athletics/) confirms campus **818 W Park Row Drive**, phone **682-867-8100**.
- [MaxPreps historical Colts seasons](https://www.maxpreps.com/tx/arlington/arlington-colts/football/history/) includes **2024 7–4** and **2025 5–4**, names coach Scott Peach for 2026, and must not be conflated with other Arlington ISD schools.
- [MaxPreps 2026 schedule](https://www.maxpreps.com/tx/arlington/arlington-colts/football/schedule/) snapshot last updated **Sept 13, 2026** shows Byron Nelson **W38–22**, V.R. Eaton **L10–20**, Red Oak **W52–47**. Because this old snapshot lacks later game finals, it is only a **2–1 partial** source, not a declared Oct. 9 season record.
- Real stadium entrance/accessible parking/tickets and legally reusable team photo were not verified; school campus address cannot substitute for a stadium entry. Production browser verification not performed.
