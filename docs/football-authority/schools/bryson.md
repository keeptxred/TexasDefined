## Bryson-specific editorial corrections

- **Mascot, colors and geography:** First-party Bryson ISD school song explicitly calls its team **Cowboys in blue and gold** (avoid the competing retail/NFL brand and football logos). NCES places Bryson ISD (TX-119901) in **Jack County**, with physical district office **300 N McCloud**, Bryson 76427, phone 940-392-3281. MaxPreps uses **305 McCloud** for its school contact, PigskinPrep **309 McCloud** for Cowboys Field; these are *not verified* stadium spectator gates, ADA parking or public entrance addresses. Original, independently drawn blue score form graphic is based on date-stamped source results, **not** school-owned photography or seals.
- **Competitive format:** Bryson now competes in **2026 six-man Class 1A Division I District 9** with Newcastle, Perrin-Whitt and Vernon Northside per UIL. Historically it appeared in the **1995 UIL Class A eleven-man** playoff archive, beat Gorman 12–8 in bi-district and lost to Hico 26–6 in area. Do not describe the 1995 game as six-man; Bryson did not earn a title from it. Six-ManFootball also records **three straight playoff years, 2015–17**, and **six consecutive district wins 2016–17** in a separately maintained historical records window; neither is a state-title appearance.
- **Coach-source dispute:** Bryson ISD officially verifies **Kevin Ray is Athletic Director**, but the current first-party personnel page does **not** say 2026 varsity head football coach. MaxPreps lists Kevin Ray football head coach, Noah Sprinkle/Justin Ramirez assistants. Six-ManFootball and PigskinPrep name **Justin Ramirez head coach**. This is a substantial conflict, so the implemented article **does not certify a head-coach name**. Instead it explains the exact disagreement in the notice, overview and FAQ and directs confirmation to the ISD athletic office.
- **2026 school-specific football:** Full dated eight-result series yields **4–4 overall as of October 16, 2026** after the **46–22 victory at Perrin-Whitt**. Early October static MaxPreps schedule had only six entries and described the 10/9 Oglesby and 10/16 Perrin-Whitt matches as unplayed, while the October 17 game recap gives 4–4 and latest win. USAPrep independently supplies all scores. The 8/28 Whiteface game **was at Jayton** per the opponent's Six-ManFootball schedule, not assumed Bryson home; attributed as neutral. The 9/4 94–71 Harrold score is a true six-man result, not a typo to normalize. A modern OnSI game result page reports 5–4 (extra incomplete match) and 2026 MaxPreps old team home 3–4; no unsupported ninth game should be inserted.
- **Upcoming:** Bryson ISD's official calendar says **Friday October 30 Vernon Northside 7:30 PM First Responders Night**. Oct 9 Oglesby was not district-nine opponent. MaxPreps and Texas Prep schedule **Friday November 6 Newcastle at Bryson 7:30 PM**; future games remain without results. Bryson has no verified 2026 online ticket provider nor stadium wheelchair entrance/access price guarantee.
