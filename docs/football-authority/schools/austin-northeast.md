## Historical Reagan-to-Northeast football authority correction (October 10, 2026)

[Northeast Early College's official school history](https://northeastechs.austinschools.org/about-us/history) confirms the school opened in 1965 as Reagan, was renamed by Austin ISD board vote in 2018, and operated as Northeast ECHS in 2019–20. [UIL football all-time appearances](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/all-time-appearances) assigns **three state titles in 1967, 1968 and 1970**, plus a fourth finals appearance in **1973**, to the same historical program. [January 30, 1969 US Congressional Record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-CRECB-1969-pt2/pdf/GPO-CRECB-1969-pt2-8-2.pdf) directly honors Reagan's back-to-back 1967/1968 football championships. The campus lineage makes these historical titles relevant to the Northeast page; neither its 2019 renaming nor its present 4A alignment changes historical results. Coaching-vacancy notices from March 2026 are not proof no head coach currently holds the job. Program-specific championship content now committed in `462cf34db70ed841527141c527666ff080217357`.
