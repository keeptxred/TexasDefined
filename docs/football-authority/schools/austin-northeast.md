## Historical Reagan-to-Northeast football authority correction (October 10, 2026)

[Northeast Early College's official school history](https://northeastechs.austinschools.org/about-us/history) confirms the school opened in 1965 as Reagan, was renamed by Austin ISD board vote in 2018, and operated as Northeast ECHS in 2019–20.
