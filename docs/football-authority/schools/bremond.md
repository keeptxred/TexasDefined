## First-party and period research
- [Bremond ISD Athletics](https://www.bremondisd.net/athletics) identifies the Fighting Tigers, 601 W Collins Street campus, athletic director **Jeff Kasowski**, a separate **2026 varsity football schedule**, and 2026–27 coaches. Its present AD listing alone does **not** prove he is 2026 football head coach.
- [UIL all-time football](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/all-time-appearances): **four state titles** (1981, 2014, 2015, 2016), **eight final appearances** overall; distinct 1983, 1987, 1993, 2007 runner-up seasons.
- [UIL 2014 official Bremond team](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/state-team/bremond-2014-2015-football) identifies 14–0 and head coach Jeff Kasowski. [UIL 2015 team](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/state-team/bremond-2015-2016-football) records 15–0, Kasowski, Tigers and red/white school colors. [UIL 2016 team](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/state-team/bremond-2016-2017-football) independently records Kasowski and 15–0 that championship season.
- [MaxPreps 2026 Bremond team page](https://www.maxpreps.com/tx/bremond/bremond-tigers/football/) shows a **7–0** snapshot through Oct 16 with the **63–0 Snook** result. Its [older schedule tab](https://www.maxpreps.com/tx/bremond/bremond-tigers/football/schedule/) remained 6–0 through Oct 9, 2026. A separate [Texas Prep Football results archive](https://txprepfootball.com/schools/bremond/2026/) disagrees on the Sept 4 Marlin game (**50–14**, while MaxPreps reports **51–14**). The editorial currently attributes that game's **51–14** reported result to MaxPreps; it is not school-certified. Follow up with first-party scorebook if possible.
- [UIL 2026–28 alignment](https://realignment.uiltexas.org/alignments/2026/2AD2FB2026.pdf) places Bremond in 2A Division II District 13.
