## Unique defects found

- The generic page did not capture Johnson's short but distinct program history, including the **2024 regional semifinal**.
- Current head coach **Sam Carlin** has a first-party Johnson High source and should replace stale staff references.
- Johnson returned to District 26-6A in 2026 and is 3-5 / 2-2 district through October 16, not the stale 1-4 record still shown in some preseason-oriented pages.
- The September 25 Hays game was the **first football meeting** between the Buda schools and the inaugural Buda Bowl.

- No school logo or third-party action photo is copied.
