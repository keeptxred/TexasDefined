## Primary and independent source trail
- [UIL all-time state football appearances](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/all-time-appearances): 12 titles in 14 state-final appearances, winner years **1998; 2009, 2010, 2011; 2013, 2014; 2016; 2018, 2019, 2020; 2022, 2023**. No subsequent title entered through research date.
- [UIL 2023–24 Aledo team archive](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/state-team-mp-archive/aledo-2023-2024-football): Bearcats, **orange and black**, head coach **Robby Jones** in 2023, pre-final 15–0 record; 2023 championship completion came next. Historic 5A Division I placement is not the current 2026 class.
- [Aledo ISD official 2026 football staff](https://www.aledoisdathletics.com/sport/football/boys/?tab=staff): head coach Jones, assistant head coach/defensive coordinator Brad McCone, offensive coordinator Joe Williams and separately listed co-coordinators.
- [UIL official 2026–28 6A realignment](https://realignment.uiltexas.org/alignments/2026/2026_RR-Packet.pdf): Class **6A District 3** includes Aledo, Arlington, Arlington Bowie, Arlington Houston/Sam Houston, Arlington Lamar, Arlington Martin, Granbury and Weatherford.
- [Aledo official 2026 varsity football schedule](https://www.aledoisdathletics.com/sport/football/boys?tab=schedule): site and venue, including Oct. 9 home vs Arlington Bowie; official future dates are subject to change. The official schedule currently has blank result entries, so it is not itself a trustworthy live W-L scoreboard.
- [Aledo ISD Buchanan Stadium official page](https://www.aledoisdathletics.com/buchanan-stadium): 1000 Bailey Ranch Road, Aledo; clear-bag policy maximum **12×6×12 inches** for transparent bag, one-gallon clear freezer bags, small clutches and medical exceptions with detailed restrictions. Official policy supersedes generic bag advice.
- [Aledo Ledger school-board record analysis](https://aledoledger.com/answers/bearcats-state-titles/): board cited 10th football championship in 2020, 12th in 2023; recognized 2025 football team as a **semifinalist**, not a 13th champion, in Jan. 2026 board notes.
- [September 25, 2026 Arlington Martin result report](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/down-bearcats-arlington-martin-ends-024149692.html): Martin 35, Aledo 21, breaks 136 consecutive district football victories. 135 was preseason count; win at Granbury advanced streak to 136 before Martin ended it.
- [Aledo football early-season MaxPreps score snapshot](https://www.maxpreps.com/tx/aledo/aledo-bearcats/football/schedule/): as of Oct. 9 shows 2–3 with win Granbury **69–0** and loss Martin **21–35**; schedules/results update and cannot be assumed stable.
- [Aledo High school 2026 graduation event page](https://ahs.aledoisd.org/parents-students/aledo-high-school-graduation-information/class-of-2026-graduation): cross-check stadium site 1000 Bailey Ranch Road but graduation-specific guest rules are not a football policy.
