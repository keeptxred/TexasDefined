## School research with preserved sources
- [UIL football all-time appearances](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/all-time-appearances): **Albany four state titles** in **nine state-final appearances** (appearance years 1954, 1960, 1961, 1991, 2009, 2014, 2015, 2022 and 2023). Championship years marked 1960, 1961, 2022 and 2023. Historic conference nomenclature is not the 2026 alignment.
- [UIL historical title results](https://www.uiltexas.org/historical-archives/athletics/archives/football/champions.html): 1960 Class 1A over **Crosby 20–0** and 1961 over **Hull-Daisetta 18–12**. Some primary tables spell Hull-Dalsetta; the result/identity are the same historical matchup.
- [UIL Albany 2022–23 state-team](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/state-team/albany-2022-2023-boys-football) and [2022 Dave Campbell's final recap](https://www.texasfootball.com/article/2022/12/14/albany-mart-state): coach **Denney Faith** led the Lions to defeat **Mart 41–21**, the first Albany football title since 1961. DCTF records Coy Lefevre, Wyatt Windham, Tye Edgar in the championship.
- [UIL 2023–24 team roster](https://www.uiltexas.org/football/state-team-mp-archive/albany-2023-2024-football): team entering state final at **15–0**, school colors **red and white**, varsity coach Denney Faith. [December 14 2023 final recap](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/texas-sports-nation/hs-sports/football/article/uil-state-football-championships-recaps-18553887.php): **Albany 28, Mart 10**, Lions conclude **16–0**, defense forced three turnovers. This is a coherent before/after timeline, not an extra title.
- [Albany ISD athletics 2026](https://www.albanyisd.net/apps/pages/index.jsp?type=d&uREC_ID=582174): coach Faith remains listed for varsity football in 2026–27 and provides separate varsity, JV, junior-high schedules and live-stream resources; school office **325-762-3974**.
- [Albany ISD official school home](https://albanyisd.net/): high school campus **501 East South First**, Albany TX 76430. [TEA 2026 Albany campus](https://rptsvr1.tea.texas.gov/cgi/sas/broker?_debug=0&_program=perfrept.perfmast.sas&_service=marykay&ccyy=2026&dds_report=D9&id=209901001&lev=C&prgopt=reports%2Facct%2Fdistinctions.sas): location **Shackelford County**.
- [UIL 2026–28 2A Division II districts](https://realignment.uiltexas.org/alignments/2026/2AD2FB2026.pdf): **District 7** Albany, Cross Plains, Goldthwaite, Hamlin, Miles, Stamford, Winters. Dated official classification, not unverified historic rivalry series.
- [Albany's first public school historical marker at the stadium](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=85275): preserved arches from 1884 educational structures, parts moved/rebuilt at gateway; intersection Central Street and North Walnut; physical town/school connection.
- [Independent Stadium Journey research](https://www.stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/robert-nail-memorial-stadium-albany-lions) and [TexasBob stadium profile](https://texasbob.com/stadium/stadium.php?id=530): historic stone entrance, Robert Nail stadium and Denney Faith Field name. The images on those sites are **not** republished without documented permission.
