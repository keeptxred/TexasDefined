## Unique defects found

- The page needed to distinguish **Jack C. Hays High School / Hays Hawks** from generic Hays County references and older school branding.
- The program moved back to **Class 6A District 26** for 2026 after recent 5A seasons.
- Mark Torres is current head coach/athletic coordinator; older Les Goad references are stale.
- The 2026 season changed quickly: Hays started 4-1, then lost to Austin High, Bowie and Dripping Springs to sit 4-4 after October 23.
- The 2020 10-4 regional-final run is a major recent benchmark but is **not a state championship**.
- Bob Shelton Stadium is a sourced football venue, but exact game-night gate/parking/accessibility details still require current event confirmation.
- The modern **Hawks** identity dates to the 2021 mascot change; older branding must not be reused.

## Source-backed implementation

- Official Hays High campus page and school history anchor campus, mascot and red/blue identity.
- Hays CISD brand guidance supports Royal Blue / Scarlet Red as the approved primary colors.
- Official athletics page identifies Mark Torres as athletic coordinator; current football sources identify him as head coach.
- 2026 ledger through October 23: wins over San Marcos, Seguin, Leander and Johnson; losses to Harker Heights, Austin High, Bowie and Dripping Springs.
- Hays Free Press documents the **first Buda Bowl** on September 25, a 41-28 Hays win over Johnson.
- Dave Campbell's documents 25 playoff appearances, one title-game appearance, zero state titles and Bob Shelton Stadium.
- Hays County reciprocal link and a source-derived football graphic are added.
