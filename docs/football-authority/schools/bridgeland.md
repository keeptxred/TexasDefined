## Source-backed historical and current facts

- CFISD identifies **Bears; Orange and Navy** as school mascot/colors. Official campus is **10707 Mason Rd, Cypress, TX 77433**, phone 832-349-7600 (not Cy-Fair FCU Stadium).
- First-party Bridgeland HS staff lists **Lonnie Madison** as **head football coach** and athletic coordinator (verified October 20, 2026).
- Dave Campbell's Texas Football preview recorded the Bears entering the **2020 Klein Collins** first-round game **10–0** and looking for their first playoff victory; the 2020–21 schedule subsequently lists a dramatic **47–44 (4OT)** win over Dekaney and a **48–24** postseason loss to Rockwall-Heath. The schedule says 12–1 overall; other history aggregators may differ, so do not invent a 2020 final ranking.
- The 2024 Bears reached a **12–2** record, and Duncanville beat them **31–13** in the **December 7, 2024 6A Division I quarterfinal**. The UIL official *state championship-game appearances* table does not list a Bridgeland appearance; **quarterfinal does not mean state final**.
- Current MaxPreps football home reports **6–1 overall / 4–0 district** through October 16, 2026. Reported finals: The Woodlands 31–30 W; Tompkins 35–7 W; Ridge Point 35–56 L; Cypress Ranch 31–14 W; Cypress Springs 37–21 W; George Ranch 49–8 W; Fulshear 49–7 W. Houston Chronicle corroborates the Fulshear final. October 23 Cypress Park and later Waller/Foster are *unplayed as of this checkpoint*.
- CFISD Berry Center's **Bridgeland vs Cypress Park** event listing directs Bridgeland home spectators to **West Road, lots E/F, entrances 5/6**; Cypress Park visitors use **Barker Cypress, lots A/H, entrances 7/8**. Venue is **Cy-Fair FCU Stadium, 8877 Barker Cypress Road, Cypress, TX 77433**, not school campus. This direction is strictly game-specific: never apply it to every away game.
- Berry Center official stadium policy describes clear bags, metal detectors, advance/same-day tickets, no re-entry, no tailgating and access information. Check individual current event status and entrances before travel.
