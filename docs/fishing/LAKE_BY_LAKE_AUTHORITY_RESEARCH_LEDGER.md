## What the source-based completion count does — and does not — certify

Each lake has an individual source-referenced record with campground or explicit no-camping findings, waterbody-specific fishing rules or a sourced statement that statewide limits apply, and genuine named day-trip activities or nearby attractions. Each entry includes a direct primary agency, government or actual commercial-operator source. **This is a research/record-coverage milestone, not a certification that all pages are ideal Google authority pages.** Campground vacancies, prices and business continuity require operator confirmation. Some records make explicit that a ramp has no campsite. Regulations and seasonal closures must be reviewed when updated. Photos, all access links, other fishing tabs, independent fact checking and final live rendering are separate QA tasks.

**Lake Conroe** is a dedicated route, not a showcase generator: its dossier is rendered via `src/data/fishing/lake-conroe-page-data.server.ts` and `LakeConroeGuide.tsx`. Other 71 lake dossiers overlay their respective individual showcase records.

## Release and maintenance safeguards

1. `npm run fishing:validate` executes source-record, URL-specific and metadata parity contracts. Keep source-generated pages not independently evidenced under existing `noindex` controls; never add boilerplate to game the depth rule.
2. Protected GitHub PR checks and production workflow are mandatory. Do not mark deployment successful until version fingerprint, canonical production health and asset checks pass.
3. An operator link is evidence, **not** a telephone verification, real-time reservation or conclusive proof of a current fee. Refresh the TPWD freshwater legal-limit summaries annually before September 1, 2027 and when TPWD changes an exception. Some 2026 park closures expire earlier.
4. Complete page-level quality acceptance by inspecting rendered unique content, verified facts and links, genuine lake imagery, county/destination cross-links, mobile responsiveness, and source freshness per lake. Retain this ledger for future reviews; don't infer human/editor signoff from successful automated checks.
