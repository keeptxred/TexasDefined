## What 'researched' means

Each completed row contains TPWD, USACE, NPS, LCRA, or municipal reference links and individually compiled campground, official fishing-regulation, and named nearby attraction details. A cited source is **not** a live phone call or real-time fee/occupancy check. 'Researched' does not certify every fishing guide section, images, mobile QA, or GSC indexing. Each source record remains independently reviewable.

Do not mark a lake researched from generated description text, an operator home page alone, duplicated summaries, a word count or generic TPWD links. Campgrounds require names and overnight details; fishing rules require waterbody-specific exceptions or a documented statement that statewide rules apply; attractions require an actual named park, trail, monument, or other site with its operator source. Preserve actual source-review dates, and recheck regulations before the next TPWD annual cycle.
