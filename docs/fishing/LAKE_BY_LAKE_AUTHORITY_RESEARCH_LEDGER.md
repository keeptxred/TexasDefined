## What 'researched' means

Each completed row has directly attributed TPWD, USACE, NPS, LCRA, or municipal reference links and a manually prepared campground, official fishing-regulation explanation, and named nearby attraction. A cited source is **not** a live phone call or real-time fee/occupancy check. 'Researched' does not certify all 8 guide sections, images, mobile QA, or GSC indexing. Each source record remains independently reviewable.

Do not mark a lake researched from a generated description, operator home page alone, duplicated summaries, a word count, or general TPWD links. For campgrounds include **name, type, overnight availability and sources**; for rules include **lake exceptions, waterbody ID and current rules cycle**, for attractions include **an actual site/park/trail and its operator source**, ideally proximity/access. Preserve source-checked dates and recheck rules annually.
