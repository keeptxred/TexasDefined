# TexasDefined indexation and ownership policy

This document records the production rule established during the August 21, 2026 thin-page cleanup.

- Keep and improve legitimate TexasDefined lifestyle, travel, outdoors, home, property, moving, culture, sports, calculator, county, and practical-service pages.
- Keep Texas DMV, driver-license, vehicle-registration, Find My DMV, and fishing-license guidance on TexasDefined because they are practical resident/outdoors service guides rather than a general government directory.
- State-agency/government authority profiles belong on KeepTXRed. Preserve useful existing TexasDefined URLs with permanent redirects when a canonical destination exists.
- Appraisal-district and county tax-office profile URLs consolidate into the applicable county property-tax guide; county-clerk profiles consolidate into the county guide; DPS-office profiles consolidate into the driver-license guide.
- Empty or inactive shop collections must not be indexable or linked as active collections. A collection requires at least one live assigned product.
- Utility states, search results, carts, checkout-return pages, and similar technical surfaces use noindex where the URL must continue to exist.
- A thin but valid editorial/tool page should be improved rather than hidden solely because of word count.
- Sitemap entries must be indexable, owned by TexasDefined, and consistent with canonical behavior. Parent-canonical utility/alias routes must not be advertised as independent sitemap canonicals.

This policy is intentionally about page purpose and canonical ownership, not a sitewide word-count threshold.
