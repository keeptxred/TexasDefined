# Citation Magnet Batches 2.16–2.19

Status: **implemented for validation**

- **2.16 — Texas small-town comparison:** the canonical `/explore/small-towns` category now compares maintained destination records by region, nearby town, season guidance, highlights, planning notes and official source without inventing a quality-of-life ranking.
- **2.17 — Texas road-trip matrix:** the canonical `/explore/road-trips` category now exposes the same structured planning matrix and explicitly avoids inferred live drive times or road conditions.
- **2.18 — Texas attractions comparison:** `/explore/attractions-comparison` provides a catalog-wide comparison of the SEO-ready/currently visitable Texas Defined destination catalog. It is explicitly scoped to the maintained catalog rather than claiming every attraction in Texas.
- **2.19 — City/county relationship dataset:** `/texas-data/city-county-relationships` exposes the current Texas Defined city registry mapped to county and region in both a table and machine-readable `Dataset`/`ItemList` schema. It states clearly that the curated city directory is not every incorporated municipality or census place.

No unsupported popularity, cost, quality-of-life or travel-time rankings are generated.
