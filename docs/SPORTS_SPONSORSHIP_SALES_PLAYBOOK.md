# TexasDefined Sports Sponsorship Sales Playbook

Updated: 2026-09-15

## Purpose

Use TexasDefined's sports-venue guides as one qualified surface inside the broader TexasDefined advertiser program. Sports sponsorship is no longer a separate low-price product. It uses the same Local, Growth, Premier and Custom commercial tiers shown on `/partner-with-us`.

Paid placements remain clearly separated from editorial recommendations, rankings, factual conclusions and structured data.

## Pre-outreach hold

**Do not send advertiser outreach until the commercial launch checklist is complete.** The site, agreement acceptance, correct live Stripe account, invoice flow, payment methods, receipt behavior and production placement rendering must all be tested first. The existing sports sponsorship server-side launch hold remains fail-closed until intentionally lifted after launch readiness is confirmed.

## Commercial terms

- Monthly Local, Growth and Premier plans begin with a three-month initial commitment.
- Annual plans are billed annually in advance and are priced at approximately ten months of the corresponding monthly rate.
- New advertisers prepay unless Texas Defined approves invoice terms in writing.
- Approved organizations may receive Net 15 terms; Net 30 can be approved when procurement requirements justify it.
- No guaranteed impression, click, booking, revenue, ranking, social-reach or editorial outcome unless a signed custom order expressly states a delivery quantity.
- Surface availability remains governed; only one approved sports sponsored placement may run on a sports surface at a time under the current sports delivery architecture.
- Sponsor approval and placement approval remain separate steps.
- Any copy revision returns a sports placement to draft for reapproval.
- Sponsored links are clearly disclosed and use appropriate sponsored/nofollow attributes.
- Aggregate impressions and clicks may be reported; visitor PII is not collected for sports sponsorship metrics.

## Qualification checklist

A sports-travel prospect should satisfy most of these:

- Has a current HTTPS website.
- Serves visitors in the same market as one or more TexasDefined sports venues.
- Can fulfill demand from game, concert or tournament weekends.
- Has a clear consumer action such as booking a room, reserving a table, requesting transportation, buying admission, reserving parking or booking a tour.
- Is compatible with Texas Defined's advertising standards.
- Would still be a legitimate reader option if it were not paying TexasDefined.
- Can be described accurately without implying TexasDefined tested, ranked or endorsed it.

## Sales positioning after launch

Lead with relevance rather than generic banner inventory. The useful question is whether a business solves a real need for a reader planning a Texas trip or event visit.

Examples:

- Hotel: relevant lodging for visitors attending an event or using a destination guide.
- Restaurant: a practical nearby option for readers already planning time in the area.
- Transportation: airport, charter, group or event transportation in the same market.
- Attraction or tour: something useful for an out-of-town visitor before or after the event.
- Parking: a service that addresses a common attendance friction point.

Never fabricate audience figures, customer counts, conversion rates, exclusivity, endorsements or "top" status.

## Common advertiser questions

### How much traffic do you get?

Use current analytics only. If the audience is still early-stage, say so and position the product around relevance, placement and founding-partner access rather than invented scale.

### Can we be listed as the best hotel or restaurant?

No. Paid relationships do not buy rankings or editorial conclusions. Sponsorship is a separate disclosed commercial placement.

### Can you guarantee bookings?

No. Texas Defined can report available aggregate placement metrics but does not guarantee bookings, revenue or other business outcomes unless a signed custom order expressly says otherwise.

### Can we have exclusivity?

The current sports architecture limits each individual sports surface to one approved sponsored placement at a time. Broader category or section exclusivity is available only when separately quoted and documented in a Custom or Premier order.

### Can we change the copy?

Yes, subject to review. Under the sports sponsorship governance system, a saved revision returns the placement to draft and requires reapproval before it can run.

## Commercial/editorial line

A sponsor may buy a disclosed placement. A sponsor may not buy:

- an editorial ranking;
- a favorable review;
- removal of a competitor from editorial coverage;
- an inaccurate claim;
- inclusion in editorial structured data as though TexasDefined recommended the business;
- fabricated traffic or performance claims;
- undisclosed commercial links.

That separation is part of the product, not an obstacle to it.
