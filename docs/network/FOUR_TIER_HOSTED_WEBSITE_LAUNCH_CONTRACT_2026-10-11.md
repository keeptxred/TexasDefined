# TexasDefined Business Network — Four-tier hosted Website launch contract

**State:** Engineering proposal, October 11, 2026. Not approved commercial terms, not customer-facing production acceptance, and **not an implemented website**. PR #4660 is unmerged. The sole TexasDefined administrator retains final authority over pricing, terms and production release. The Network launches only once, with all four plans independently tested and operational.

## Commercial architecture under consideration

| Tier | Proposed monthly price | Actual customer value |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Basic | Free | Reviewed, searchable business listing hosted on TexasDefined, containing business identity, location/service area, hours, description and phone |
| Plus | $19.99 (already configured in Stripe live) | Expanded hosted profile with services, FAQ, gallery, optional external website/social links and a privacy-preserving 30-day observed listing/contact summary |
| Featured | $49.95 (proposed; no live Checkout) | Plus, separately reviewed owner updates, **clearly labeled sponsored directory placement**, and expanded daily/channel reporting; sponsored stories priced separately |
| **Website** | **$79.95 (proposed; no live Checkout)** | Everything in Featured **plus** a professionally assembled, limited-scope, multi-page business website hosted under a TexasDefined path, intended for organizations without their own website |

**These packages are not interchangeable.** Featured buys more discovery within the directory, **not** a five-page website. The $79.95 service sells an online home with real site navigation and bounded setup/update assistance. No existing website is required. A business with an external website can still join any tier.

## Website tier proposed scope and hard limits

1. **Up to five real, separately addressable, internally navigable pages per business**, with a consistent mobile-first design: Home, About, Services, Gallery and Contact; omit irrelevant pages rather than publishing empty or boilerplate content. An approved site may use fewer pages. The pages must have distinct titles, canonicals, crawlable links and meaningful original content, not only in-page anchors or clones of the directory profile.
2. **One initial website assembly included per newly approved subscription**, from business-submitted facts, licensed business images and one curated design layout with approved colors/logo. Launch only after independent business ownership checks, editorial accuracy review, and explicit publication. No unbounded design iteration, copywriting project or unlimited import.
3. **One logo and up to 12 approved photo assets** across the site, including the existing public business-media storage system where compatible. No remote arbitrary script/code uploads, external unverified CDN sources, or unlimited unreviewed storage. File count, size, optimized derivatives, atomic quota and immutable review copies must be enforced server-side/DB; the current six-photo listing cap is **not** proof of 12-photo Website support.
4. **Up to two content-update requests per paid billing month.** Each request modifies content on **one existing page** or replaces **up to three** approved photos, not an entire redesign, new page or website migration. No promises of instant turnaround; final review turnaround and carryover/expiry need sole-owner commercial approval.
5. **Contact page** with validated click-to-call and click-to-email links, hours/service area, and official map/directions where reliable. A hosted inquiry/contact form, if included in the final advertised package, requires rate-limiting, anti-spam, consent/retention, owner-bound delivery and Microsoft Graph end-to-end evidence. No form submission or lead forwarding may be advertised until tested.
6. **SEO and accessibility:** per-page unique descriptions, titles, canonicals, appropriate LocalBusiness/Organization structured data only when backed by published information, real 404 for unpublished pages, alt text, sitemap after launch, noindex staging, responsive and keyboard-accessible navigation, production source render and hydration checks. Never guarantee rankings, clicks, unique visitors, appointments, revenue or inclusion in Discover.
7. **Hosting scope:** URL is a path under `texasdefined.com` with a stable editorially reviewed business slug. No separate hosting bill for that TexasDefined web presence, **but the membership is $79.95/month**, subject to approval. No custom domain, domain registration, DNS, branded email/mailbox, arbitrary website builder, ecommerce, business-customer Checkout, appointment booking, off-site ad spend, unlimited pages/images or support SLA unless separately contracted and implemented.
8. **Ownership and cancellation:** business retains rights to its own submitted content, while TexasDefined retains its platform/domain/templates; terms must specify accessible content export, portability, correction procedure, cancellation effective date, deletion/retention/appeal holds, domain-URL stability, suspension behavior and no automatic republication when payment recovers.

## Non-negotiable system architecture before accepting a Website subscriber

- **Verified owner + editorial gate:** use the existing single-admin approval and independently verified business identity; two separate authenticated synthetic business owners must be tested. Private drafts and unverified owners never become publicly readable.
- **Distinct tier and subscription:** add a versioned pending schema migration supporting `website` in application, owner and published plan policies, with secure verified transitions. Add independent $79.95 live/test Stripe products/prices, Checkout, Billing Portal, webhook price checks, idempotency, invoice/customer identity, failed-payment suspension, upgrade/downgrade and termination handling. Preserve the existing Plus $19.99 and proposed Featured $49.95 as separate entitlements. Never coerce a Website subscription into Plus or issue its benefits based on client-input plan strings.
- **Actual site storage/render:** new versioned, RLS-protected page records (one app/site, max five published page slugs, draft revisions separate), page-specific editorial approval, immutable media, atomic media limits, no arbitrary HTML/JS execution, and stable routes e.g. `/network/site/{business-slug}/{page}`. The precise path may be finalized in source, but it must remain under TexasDefined. At least five-page and image-quota boundary concurrency tests; reject unknown paths and stale/unpublished revisions.
- **Reporting:** per-business anonymous aggregate page/display and contact actions with truthful counting; customer-only authorization, explicit tier reporting, defined 35-day retention and scheduled deletion. No persisted IP, device ID, visitor identity, raw browsing history or owner email in metrics.
- **Operational safety:** scoped Graph messages, mailbox restrictions and independently confirmed delivery; payment/owner revocation immediately stops unreviewed page visibility; emergency unpublish must remain available. Hosted stage stays separate from production; never create billable branches without express permission.

**Staging isolation:** Use a separate staging Supabase project (never the existing production project), a nonproduction Workers hostname, synthetic authenticated owners, Stripe test-mode billing and the controlled administrator mail sink. Do not provision a chargeable stage without explicit cost approval.

## One-launch acceptance and evidence

**A new fifteenth category, `hostedWebsiteDelivered`, is added to `NETWORK_LAUNCH_EVIDENCE_KEYS`.** It must be supported by real two-owner staging/browser/database/Stripe evidence in addition to the existing fourteen categories. Do not mark this category passed based on CI, mocked fixtures, a pretty screenshot, a UI price card, a manually invented reference or a provider-free prototype.

Minimum independently verifiable proof: all four tier application/onboarding journeys; a published five-page Website with correct accessible desktop/mobile navigation, media cap and two edit requests; approved versus unapproved content visibility; two-owner RLS/Auth/Storage isolation; correct published-only SEO/sitemap and staging noindex; separate Stripe test-mode $49.95/$79.95 billing and rebinding/failure/recovery/cancellation; genuine Graph and Cron delivery; rollback and content portability; reviewed tax/refund/renewal terms; sole-owner final acceptance. The underlying fourteen launch categories remain fully binding.

**Production release rule:** Do not merge or deploy the complete Network until the full Website experience, Featured, Plus, Basic, provider flows, and all fifteen evidence categories pass on one exact reconciled commit. Do not solve incomplete Website features by leaving the tier disabled in the final release. Until then, a **planned/pricing-inquiry** description in an unmerged draft is not a functioning service or authorization to charge.

**Final commercial decisions still required:** precise name (“Website”/“Managed Website”), $79.95 amount, one-time setup fee (currently propose $0), final content/photo/edit limits, contact-form scope, cancellation and data export, tax treatment, content-licensing terms, permitted categories, sponsor placement policy, and final owner approval.
