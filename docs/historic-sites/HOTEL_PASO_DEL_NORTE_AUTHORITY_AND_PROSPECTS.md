Reviewed: 2026-10-17 · Path: /destination/hotel-paso-del-norte

## Canonical identity and historical facts
- Canonical title: Hotel Paso del Norte, El Paso — historic hotel, designed by Trost & Trost.
- Opening: Thanksgiving 1912, per the hotel's [official chronology](https://www.hotelpdn.com/history).
- National Register: January 5, 1979, #79002933, architectural/commercial significance, per [THC Atlas](https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/Details?atlasnumber=2079002933) and [NPS NRIS](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/79002933).
- Official historic-register address: **115 El Paso Street**. Current operator and [Visit El Paso](https://visitelpaso.com/places/hotel-paso-del-norte) direct arriving guests to **10 Henry Trost Court, El Paso, TX 79901**. Never present the historic address as modern check-in instructions.
- [THC preservation case study](https://www.thc.texas.gov/public/upload/Architecture_Committee_2-2-2021.pdf): original Trost design, 2020 rehabilitation and historic lobby/dome preservation. The dome is described by the operator as **Tiffany-style**; no unsupported attribution of manufacture to Tiffany Studios.
- Price, amenity availability, dining schedules, event access and accessibility must be confirmed with the hotel at booking. The guide does not claim the hotel is a public museum.
