## Code and editorial completeness controls

- Existing main branch short aliases remain **single-canonical** 301 redirects. Do not create duplicate destination records. Review visitor fact dates only after direct factual verification.
- Capitol: 6-event cited chronology, 3 visitor-fact sources, 3 distinct itineraries, two additional licensed photos and multiple verified Austin companion links.
- LBJ: 9 focused topic/exhibit blurbs, 10 researched on-page FAQs with question provenance, 3 study/visit routes, 4 sourced visitor-fact cards, two additional archival public-domain photos and operator comparison updated in `docs/museum-official-site-benchmarks.json`.
- San José: 7-event colonial/conservation chronology, new 1794–1824 primary source analysis, 7 source-backed FAQs, 4 fact cards, two additional licensed photos and clear differentiation of park compound vs trail vs living parish.
- Every critical NPS, SPB and LBJ primary source is already in `scripts/data/authority/monitor-operator-sources.mjs` or was added this revision. The Monday GitHub schedule checks source drift and LBJ temporary exhibit deadlines; it **never blindly certifies** changed facts or bumps editorial review dates.

## Release gates — not satisfied merely by merging

1. Required CI, schema/typecheck, curation, images and historical facts test pass. Fix failures rather than disable them.
2. Main SHA is confirmed deployed by the strict GitHub-managed production workflow; no bypass of its asset-retention or worker health gates and no Cloudflare credential refresh loop.
3. Independently retrieve canonical **HTTP 200**, appropriate indexability and self-canonical for all three, aliases **301**, source links, mobile headings, image URLs/ALT and JSON-LD/sitemap.
4. Compare public text with the new 2026/1794/parking-hours/compound-gate sections; a repository file alone is **not** evidence of publication.
5. Final authority certification only after this inspection. Google has no official numerical authority score and no site can promise ranking or policy approval.
