# Christmas calendar commercial and editorial policy (2026–27)

Verified 2026 calendar entries are editorial resources, not advertisements. The 250-place
intake is source-checked one event at a time. Unannounced, stale, duplicate or
otherwise unverified entries must not receive scheduled calendar dates or SEO
authority pages. Corrected operating windows are displayed in the statewide
calendar, and evergreen authority pages are reserved for major destinations.

## AdSense approval and ad-unit guard

**AdSense is not approved** for TexasDefined as reported by the site owner on
October 23, 2026. Do not activate Google AdSense display units, insert the
adsbygoogle publisher JavaScript in holiday routes, or forecast Christmas
display-ad revenue. Existing publisher verification metadata or ads.txt alone
do not establish approval or imply that live display advertising is earning
money. After an actual approval is independently confirmed, any future ad
integration requires consent review, official publisher configuration,
performance impact testing, editorial density limits and a new gated PR.

## Revenue streams that are available to use now

1. **Hotels.com / CJ lodging referral:** one clearly labeled optional hotel
   comparison call to action in the Christmas collection, with the existing
   site-approved referral path. Event admission is never implied.
2. **Viator / destination activities:** first-party-reviewed market inventory
   only (for example Austin, San Antonio, Dallas, Galveston and Houston).
   Use the existing verified destination URLs and affiliate encoder. Display
   at most one destination discovery offer per city on the Christmas collection.
   Tours are separate from event tickets; never fabricate a product or use a
   city URL as proof of admission to a specific festival.
3. **Ticketmaster:** use the existing verified Ticketmaster event engine and
   publisher tracking for qualifying performances; never wrap an arbitrary
   official event page with the Ticketmaster affiliate encoder.
4. **Direct local seasonal advertisers:** link hotels, restaurants, visitor
   businesses and attractions to the existing advertising inquiry form,
   passing the Christmas source path. No unapproved sponsor placements or
   fabricated impressions, pageviews, bookings or earnings. Mark any
   future paid placement separately and label its commercial relationship.

## Measurement and governance

Preserve `data-commercial-partner`, `data-commercial-placement`,
`data-affiliate-partner`, and `data-affiliate-placement` on eligible
referrals so the shared first-party analytics system can measure the
impression, click and destination per placement. Enforce
`rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer"` for external affiliate
offers. Disclose possible commissions next to commercial links. Direct
advertiser inquiry links are not affiliate links.

Commercial agreements cannot purchase a verified calendar date, a favorable
editorial ranking, an authority-page designation or a falsified user review.
Avoid filling the calendar with repetitive sponsor units. The canonical
Christmas hub, statewide date calendar and county guides should crosslink
to source-backed entries and major attraction guides. Run the Christmas
validator and canonical premerge checks before deployment, and verify
production before calling a commercial change live.

Source-qualification and partner readiness must be repeated for the next
season; never silently roll 2026 operating dates into 2027 or 2028.
