# Texas Defined Integrated Campaign — Order Schedule Draft

**For attorney review before first commercial use.** This schedule supplements the Texas Defined Advertising & Sponsorship Agreement, version 2026-09-16-v2. If this schedule conflicts with the master agreement, the signed order form controls only for the expressly negotiated campaign details.

## Published launch offer

- **Integrated Campaign:** starting at **$1,500**.
- One-time custom campaign combining multiple approved Texas Defined commercial formats.
- New advertisers are prepaid by default. Campaigns under $2,500 are 100% prepaid unless Texas Defined expressly approves invoice terms.
- Campaign does not begin until required payment, approved credit terms or an approved purchase order is in place.

## Typical scope

An Integrated Campaign may combine agreed contextual sponsor placements, featured hub/section placement, destination or venue modules, a clearly disclosed sponsored feature when editorially appropriate, and specifically agreed social-support placements. Every deliverable must be written into the order form; no format is implied merely because it is listed here.

## Required order-form fields

The signed order form must identify:

1. advertiser legal name and billing contact;
2. campaign name, objective and approved destination URL(s);
3. campaign start and end dates;
4. each approved placement and deliverable;
5. sponsored-feature scope, if any, including disclosure treatment;
6. approved social-support deliverables, if any;
7. creative/assets, approvals and deadlines;
8. final campaign price, billing schedule and invoice/PO terms;
9. reporting cadence and specifically agreed delivery measurements, if any; and
10. negotiated additions, category limitations or exclusivity, if any.

## Editorial independence and sponsored content

Advertising does not purchase favorable editorial coverage, reviews, rankings, recommendations or changes to factual conclusions. A sponsored feature is commercial content and will be clearly disclosed. Texas Defined retains approval over placement, labeling, factual accuracy, legal compliance and suitability.

## Performance and make-goods

Texas Defined does not guarantee traffic, impressions, clicks, bookings, leads, conversions, sales, search rankings or editorial outcomes unless the signed order form expressly states a specific delivery quantity. If Texas Defined fails to deliver a specifically agreed placement for reasons within its control, the make-good remedies in the master agreement apply.

## Creative and advertiser responsibilities

Advertiser is responsible for the accuracy and substantiation of supplied claims and for having all necessary intellectual-property, trademark, publicity and other rights in supplied assets. Texas Defined may reject or request revision of unlawful, deceptive or unsuitable materials. Advertiser-caused delays do not automatically extend campaign dates or create a refund right.

## Signatures

This schedule becomes binding only when incorporated into a completed Texas Defined order form and accepted under the master agreement’s electronic-signature terms.
