# Texas Defined Custom Partnership — Tier Schedule

Incorporated into the **Texas Defined Advertising and Sponsorship Agreement — Base Terms**, version 2026-09-15-v1.

## Package
**Custom Partnership**

## Pricing
Custom quote stated in the signed insertion order or campaign order form.

## Commitment
Defined in the signed insertion order or campaign order form.

## Potential services
A Custom Partnership may include any approved combination of:
- Custom sponsored placement mix and campaign term.
- Custom creative and sponsored-content scope.
- Event or seasonal campaign placement.
- Section or category sponsorship.
- Optional category or section exclusivity when available.
- Custom reporting cadence.
- Social support when specifically included.
- Invoice and purchase-order support for approved accounts.

## Best fit
Tourism organizations, agencies, larger hospitality groups, statewide brands, event operators and advertisers with a campaign that does not fit the fixed Local, Growth or Premier schedule.

## Required order form
No Custom Partnership is complete without a written insertion order or order form that states the approved scope, price, campaign dates, billing terms and any special deliverables. If the order form conflicts with the Base Terms on a campaign-specific business term, the signed order form controls for that term only.

## Performance
Unless the signed order form expressly states a guaranteed delivery quantity, this package does not guarantee impressions, clicks, leads, bookings, sales, rankings, social reach, conversion rates or editorial coverage.
