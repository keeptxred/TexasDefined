# Texas Defined Premier Partner — Tier Schedule

Incorporated into the **Texas Defined Advertising and Sponsorship Agreement — Base Terms**, version 2026-09-15-v1.

## Package
**Premier Partner**

## Pricing
- Monthly: **$999/month**
- Annual: **$9,990/year**

## Commitment
- Monthly billing: three-month initial commitment, then month-to-month.
- Annual billing: twelve-month term, paid annually in advance unless approved invoice terms state otherwise.

## Included services
- Up to six approved sponsored placements on relevant Texas Defined surfaces.
- Up to two featured partner placements on relevant hubs when inventory permits.
- Up to six advertiser destination links.
- Monthly creative refreshes.
- Monthly performance summary.
- Up to two coordinated social support placements per month when campaign creative is suitable.
- Up to one clearly labeled sponsored feature per quarter, subject to editorial/commercial review.
- Optional category exclusivity when separately quoted and available.
- Priority campaign setup and placement review.

## Best fit
Statewide travel brands, larger hospitality groups, relocation brands and established Texas-serving companies that need a broader, sustained presence across tightly matched Texas Defined audiences.

## Additional terms
Specific page URLs, hub placements, sponsored-feature scope, social timing, launch dates, creative, destination URLs and any approved substitutions are documented in the campaign order or written campaign confirmation. Category exclusivity is not included unless expressly quoted and accepted in writing. This package does not guarantee impressions, clicks, leads, bookings, sales, rankings, social reach or editorial coverage.
