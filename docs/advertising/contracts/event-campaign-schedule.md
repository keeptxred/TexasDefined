# Texas Defined Event Campaign — Order Schedule Draft

**For attorney review before first commercial use.** This schedule supplements the Texas Defined Advertising & Sponsorship Agreement, version 2026-09-16-v2. If this schedule conflicts with the master agreement, the signed order form controls only for the expressly negotiated campaign details.

## Published launch offer

- **Event Campaign:** starting at **$495**.
- One-time campaign; exact price, dates, placements and deliverables must be stated on the signed order form.
- New advertisers and one-time campaigns under $2,500 are **100% prepaid** unless Texas Defined expressly approves invoice terms.
- Campaign does not begin until required payment, approved credit terms or an approved purchase order is in place.

## Typical scope

An Event Campaign may include an agreed combination of contextual event-page sponsorship, destination/venue placement, event-calendar support or other event-related inventory that Texas Defined approves in writing. Nothing in this schedule promises editorial coverage or inclusion in editorial rankings, recommendations or factual conclusions.

## Required order-form fields

The signed order form must identify:

1. advertiser legal name and billing contact;
2. event/campaign name and approved destination URL(s);
3. campaign start and end dates;
4. exact placement(s) and any quantity stated as a deliverable;
5. approved creative/assets and creative deadlines;
6. final campaign price and payment terms;
7. approved social-support deliverables, if any;
8. any approved make-good measurement; and
9. any negotiated additions.

## Delivery, disclosure and performance

Paid placements will be clearly disclosed as sponsored/partner/advertising content as appropriate. Sponsored links may use `rel="sponsored"` and/or `nofollow`. Texas Defined does not guarantee traffic, impressions, clicks, bookings, leads, conversions, sales, search rankings or editorial outcomes unless the signed order form expressly states a specific delivery quantity. If Texas Defined fails to deliver a specifically agreed placement for reasons within its control, the make-good remedies in the master agreement apply.

## Creative and timing

Advertiser must provide accurate claims, rights-cleared creative and required approvals by the stated deadline. Texas Defined may reject or request revision of creative that is unlawful, misleading, incompatible with site standards or otherwise unsuitable. Advertiser-caused delays do not automatically extend event dates or create a refund right.

## Signatures

This schedule becomes binding only when incorporated into a completed Texas Defined order form and accepted under the master agreement’s electronic-signature terms.
