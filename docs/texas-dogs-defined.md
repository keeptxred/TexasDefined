# Texas Dogs Defined

Texas Dogs Defined is the dog-life editorial and commerce-support vertical within TexasDefined.com.

## Public structure

- `/dogs` is the canonical section hub.
- `/dogs/:breed` is the permanent breed layer.
- Breed pages lead with recognizable breed personality and Texas-life context, not product inventory.
- Future merchandise should attach to the most relevant breed or editorial page instead of creating isolated SEO landing pages.

## Launch collections

The initial reusable design families are:

1. Retro Dogs
2. Distinguished Dogs
3. Dogs With Jobs
4. Dogs With Attitude
5. Texas Dogs
6. Lake Dogs
7. Seasonal Dogs

These are creative/merchandise families, not substitutes for editorial pages.

## Editorial model

The publishing queue lives in `src/data/texas-dogs-editorial-plan.ts` and separates stories into `research`, `brief-ready`, and `draft-next` states.

Stories involving changing rules, access, events, animal safety, shelters, beaches, parks, or other time-sensitive facts must be verified against current first-party or otherwise authoritative sources before publication. Humor and breed-culture pieces can move faster but still need to meet the Texas Defined editorial style and internal-linking standards.

Every public dog story should naturally connect readers back to `/dogs`, relevant TexasDefined travel/lifestyle coverage, and—when genuinely useful—the appropriate breed or merchandise collection.

## Search and discovery

The dog hub and launch breed pages use canonical metadata, breadcrumbs, structured data, internal navigation, and sitemap inclusion. New breed records should be added through the shared dog registry so the public hub, breed routing, and sitemap remain synchronized.

## Commerce rule

Editorial usefulness comes first. Etsy or other store links should be contextual calls to action, not the reason a page exists. A product can extend a breed joke or story; it should not turn Texas Dogs Defined into a thin product catalog.
