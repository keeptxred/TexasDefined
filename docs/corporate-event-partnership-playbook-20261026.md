# TexasDefined Corporate Events — operator partnership readiness playbook (2026-10-26)

**Internal planning material only. NOT a public rate card, a venue partnership, a signed agreement, an approved offer, a quote, or an outreach log. Do not publish or send without an independent human review.** Related tracking: [#5719](https://github.com/keeptxred/TexasDefined/issues/5719).

## Purpose and honest starting position

TexasDefined has a source-reviewed corporate-event editorial directory and private tools for organizers, partner applications, contact-path research, follow-ups, offers and outcome attribution. Editorial inclusion **does not** indicate a venue partnership, guaranteed capacity, a available date, or commercial permission to route leads. Genuine inventory, consent and scope-specific signed terms must come from the venue and organizer, not from the directory. No commissions, discounts, referral relationships, conversions, bookings or revenue may be inferred from editorial coverage.

Use this playbook for small Houston-area group-experience businesses first. A 13–45 attendee request is the initial operational fit; larger groups require an explicit format-specific inquiry. Expand to other Texas markets only as confirmed organizer demand and capacity justify it. The existing [Houston source review](./corporate-events-houston-growth-20261024.md), [large-group support guide](./corporate-event-large-group-support-20261024.md), public [corporate events marketplace](https://texasdefined.com/corporate-events), and staff-only source-reviewed operator contact paths in `src/data/corporate-event-public-operator-channels.ts` are starting materials. Reverify any external links and current group policies **at the time of actual use**.

## A fair initial commercial conversation

**Offer to explore** (not a currently sold product): a source-reviewed editorial listing (independent of paid placement), an opt-in organizer inquiry path, and separately negotiated commercial promotion where relevant. Ask the venue what it can support; do not presume it agrees to send a discount, pay a referral fee, provide direct inventory, or advertise.

Before drafting an actual proposal, establish:

1. **Identity:** real business owner or authorized representative, separately independently verified business identity with nonfuture dated source evidence.
2. **Real scope:** exactly which venue/location and event formats the operator can service; simultaneous and rotated participant limits, age requirements, private room/buyout terms and event dates.
3. **Organizer rights:** contact only for the organizer's expressly authorized venue(s), after consent; revocation blocks further disclosure or quote preparation.
4. **Commercial authority:** written signed scope distinguishes *offer display*, *referral/quote routing*, *sponsorship*, and *commission*. Do not equate one scope with another. Verify effective dates, expiry, withdrawal, legal disclosures and any agreed redemption endpoint.
5. **Attribution:** independently verifiable referral identifiers and authorized confirmation, never a click counted as a booking or an assumed payout.
6. **Operations:** who receives real inquiries, who acknowledges them, follow-up expectation, venue's own official quote and booking procedure, dispute/cancellation handling.

Possible offer structures **for discussion only**, subject to operator quote and signed authorization:

- **Editorial-only** — sourced public directory coverage with no promised placement or lead delivery.
- **Verified group inquiry** — organizer-selected venue introduction under explicit consent plus authorized staff review; terms negotiated separately.
- **Explicitly approved promotion** — optional short-lived group package, credit or discount with operator-supplied terms, restrictions, expiry and signed promotional scope.
- **Distinct sponsorship** — independently labeled sponsorship with negotiated price and location, never covertly affecting the editorial ranking.

**No initial dollar rate, percentage, commission, discount, conversion target or guaranteed number of inquiries is established here.** Especially at current low measured demand, do not imply that TexasDefined can guarantee exposure, inquiries, bookings or return on ad spend. Do not pitch a “20-day risk-free trial” or other fixed test outcome without genuine operator agreement and measurable supply.

## Private operational handoff — actual events only

- **Prospect researched:** staff can open official first-party inquiry pages from the verified channel registry; clicking alone is not outreach. Preserve original source URL and review date.
- **Contact drafted:** leave outreach status unsent. **Record “sent” only after verified external correspondence exists**, with its independently reviewable source evidence and proper consent.
- **Venue expresses interest:** invite its authorized representative to the existing protected partner application/verification process. Do not auto-approve an application from an email reply or from a directory listing.
- **Organizer inquiry:** validate real future-or-current Texas event dates, group size and consent; accepted submissions must be durably stored before reporting success. Private contact remains staff-restricted.
- **Operator triage:** use the existing response-SLA, duplicate and follow-up tools; do not infer a completed contact from an opened invitation draft. Contact organizers only according to their consent and applicable requirements.
- **Venue sharing / quote:** require explicit venue-specific sharing consent still active **at the time of access**, plus verified business, signed scope-appropriate agreement and active approved offer where required.
- **Outcome:** record only externally evidenced inquiry response, genuine confirmed booking, cancellation, invoice or legitimate monetary outcome. Pageviews, listings, clicks and estimated illustrative budgets are not contracts, revenue or commissions.
- **Withdrawals or expiry:** immediately stop commercial display/routing as applicable, and never carry a past promotion forward as an active offer.

## Acceptance and business dependencies

**Engineering checks** are not a substitute for a live consenting customer and a responding human operator. Continue nonintrusive negative-path and private-role tests with isolated fixtures, never fabricated production submissions or agreements. The live mobile/tablet/desktop Chrome/axe acceptance is an *automated* baseline only; it does not prove manual WCAG compliance or complete black-box endpoint security.

The following outcomes are **not complete without genuine independent evidence**:

- A willing, independently verified authorized venue contact and an actual signed, scope-specific commercial agreement.
- A genuinely consenting organizer's successful request **and** independently observed protected staff receipt.
- Approved/current public commercial offers, if any; real redemption, bookings and financial attribution only after actual activity.
- Individually sourced venue entrance or premises geolocation and separately approved current Census ZCTA membership/representative point before a public ZIP-radius filter. Research coordinates, USPS ZIPs, Census interpolated street points and city centers are not interchangeable.

At the October 26 source checkpoint recorded in master issue #5719, live ledgers had no genuine organizer applications, partner submissions, outreach, offers, outcomes or paid commission evidence. Do not represent them as populated based on mock tests. Keep #5719 open until actual external dependencies and separate assurance work are satisfied.
