Why The Original Ninfa's matters

The Original Ninfa's on Navigation is a Houston Tex-Mex landmark rooted in the Laurenzo family's tortilla business in the East End. The restaurant's own history dates the turning point to 1973, when Ninfa Laurenzo began grilling skirt steak and serving it in handmade flour tortillas from the family's struggling tortilla factory.

Those tacos al carbon became closely associated with the spread of fajitas as a restaurant staple. The broader history of grilled skirt steak is older and more complicated than any one restaurant, but Ninfa's documented role in popularizing the dish in Houston makes the Navigation location an important stop for understanding modern Tex-Mex.

What defines the stop

The value of the original Navigation location is the connection between place, family history and a dish that moved from a local East End restaurant into national restaurant culture. That makes it more than a generic Tex-Mex listing.

Build the visit into a Houston food day rather than relying on a statewide ranking. The restaurant publishes current operating information directly; check that source before traveling because service details can change.
