Why Joe's Bakery matters

Joe's Bakery & Coffee Shop is an East Austin institution with family food-business roots reaching back to 1935, when Sophia De La O and Florentino De La O opened La Oriental Grocery & Bakery. The family later shifted the business to East 7th Street, where Joe Avila and his family developed the bakery-and-restaurant identity that continues today.

The James Beard Foundation named Joe's Bakery a 2023 America's Classics winner, highlighting its breakfast tacos, pan dulce, migas, carne guisada and multigenerational community role. That recognition makes Joe's useful not only as a breakfast stop but as a living piece of East Austin food history.

What defines the stop

Breakfast tacos are the clearest reason to connect Joe's to the wider Texas food map, but the bakery counter and neighborhood history matter just as much. The restaurant's story ties Mexican-American baking, family recipes and East Austin community life together in one place.
