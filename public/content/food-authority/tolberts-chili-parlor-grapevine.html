Why Tolbert's matters

Tolbert's Restaurant & Chili Parlor carries the food legacy of journalist and historian Frank X. Tolbert, who co-founded the Terlingua Championship Chili Cook-Off in 1967. The first Tolbert's restaurant opened in Dallas in 1976; the family brought the concept to Grapevine's historic Main Street in 2006.

That lineage gives the restaurant a direct connection to the modern mythology of Texas chili. Rather than treating chili only as a recipe, Tolbert's connects the Bowl of Red to cook-off culture, Texas journalism and the long-running argument over what belongs in a Texas chili pot.

What defines the stop

The signature reason to include Tolbert's in a food trip is its chili tradition and association with Terlingua. Grapevine also makes the stop easy to pair with a historic downtown visit instead of building an itinerary around a single meal.
