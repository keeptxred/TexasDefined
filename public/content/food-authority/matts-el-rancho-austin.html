Why Matt's El Rancho matters

Matt's El Rancho began in 1952 when Matt and Janie Martinez opened a small restaurant on East 1st Street in Austin. The family's official history reaches further back to Matt selling tamales near the Texas Capitol as a child in 1923, connecting the restaurant to a longer Mexican-American food story in the city.

The restaurant moved to its current South Lamar location in 1986 and remains family operated. Its longevity, handmade-food tradition and association with dishes such as the Bob Armstrong Dip make it a durable Austin Tex-Mex institution rather than a recent nostalgia concept.

Use Matt's to connect Tex-Mex with Austin history

TexasDefined treats Tex-Mex as its own regional food tradition, shaped by Mexican-American families, local ingredients and restaurant culture. Matt's gives that story a clear Austin anchor alongside Houston's Original Ninfa's and Dallas's El Fenix.

For a food-focused Austin day, pair the restaurant with neighborhood, music or history stops rather than scheduling multiple heavy meals back to back. Current hours, reservations and menu details should be confirmed directly with the restaurant.
