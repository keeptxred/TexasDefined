Why Snow's BBQ matters

Snow's BBQ opened in Lexington on March 1, 2003, after owner Kerry Bexley and pitmaster Tootsie Tomanetz built a restaurant around a local Saturday-barbecue tradition. The business began as a small-town operation and became a destination after statewide attention brought travelers to Lexington specifically for the food and the pit crew behind it.

Snow's is important to a Texas food map because it shows that barbecue authority is not limited to century-old meat markets. Its reputation grew from a living community custom, a distinctive once-a-week rhythm and the craft of a pitmaster whose work became closely associated with Lexington itself.

Plan it as a Lexington destination, not a casual meal slot

The restaurant's Saturday service pattern and sellout model make planning part of the experience.
