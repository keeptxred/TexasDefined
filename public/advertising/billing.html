Payment methods

How advertisers can pay

Card

Credit or debit card through a secure Stripe-hosted checkout or hosted invoice page. Texas Defined does not ask advertisers to email raw card information.

Bank / ACH

Eligible advertisers may pay by supported bank-transfer or ACH methods when those options are enabled on the Stripe-hosted invoice.

Invoice

Approved organizations can receive a formal invoice with campaign details, due date and payment link. Purchase-order requirements can be noted during contracting.

Standard policy

When payment is due

New advertisers

  • Prepay by default before the campaign begins.
  • One-time campaigns under $2,500 generally require full payment before launch.
  • Monthly sponsorships are billed monthly in advance.
  • Annual sponsorships are billed annually in advance.

Approved invoice accounts

  • Net 15 may be approved for established organizations.
  • Net 30 may be considered when an agency, government entity, tourism organization or corporate procurement process requires it.
  • A valid purchase order can be incorporated when required.
  • Campaign launch may be held until required payment or approved credit terms are in place.

Invoices

What appears on the invoice

The commercial record should identify the advertiser, selected package or campaign, service period, amount, due date and any purchase-order reference. Custom placements and special deliverables belong in the signed order form as well.

Example invoice detail

Growth Partner — Q4 campaign

Campaign period: Oct. 1–Dec. 31
Billing: Monthly in advance
Package: Growth Partner
Monthly amount: $499
Payment terms: Due on receipt

Demonstration only. Actual invoices are issued only after advertiser approval and contracting.

Security note. Payment pages should be opened from the secure Stripe-hosted link on the invoice or checkout request. Texas Defined staff will not ask an advertiser to send a full card number by email.

Available payment methods may vary by advertiser, invoice, country, bank eligibility and the payment options enabled on the applicable Stripe account.
